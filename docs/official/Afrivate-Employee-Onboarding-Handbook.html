Welcome to Afrivate

This handbook is a simplified orientation guide for new and existing staff. It summarizes culture, communication, performance, and conduct expectations.

Note: The handbook does not replace the SWP. In the event of conflict, the SWP prevails.

1. Mission & Culture Overview

Overview of Afrivate’s mission and culture: We are building the future of user connectivity. Our culture is rooted in excellence, ownership, professionalism, and high-speed execution.

2. Schedule & Availability

Summary of work schedules and availability expectations:

  • Official Days: Monday to Thursday.
  • Friday to Sunday: Off-days / Asynchronous updates.
  • Core Hours: You must be reachable and active during team-defined hours.

3. Communication Standards

Communication rules and response time standards:

  • The 4-Hour Rule: Official messages received through ClickUp must be acknowledged within four hours.
  • Official Communication Channel: ClickUp is used for internal communication, coordination, clarification, and follow-up.
  • Portal System of Record: Use the AfriVate Portal to complete and record every workflow available within it, including tasks, weekly check-ins, leave, onboarding, goals, learning submissions, surveys, feedback, policy acknowledgements, resources, events, and people operations.
  • Email: Use your @afrivate email for account access and approved external or formal correspondence. It does not replace ClickUp for internal communication.

4. Performance & Reporting

KPI and reporting expectations:

  • Every employee maintains 3–5 Weekly KPIs.
  • Agree goals with your Team Lead and record them through Portal → Growth → OKRs.
  • Submit your weekly report through Portal → Weekly check-in; your Team Lead will review it there.
  • Keep assigned Portal tasks accurate by updating status, progress, hours, and blockers.
  • Goal: Support the target of 1,000,000 users by Dec 31, 2026.

5. Evaluation & Conduct

High-level appraisal and discipline overview:

  • Appraisals are 60% Output and 40% Soft Skills.
  • Afrivate uses a progressive discipline system (Warnings → PIP → Termination).

Code of Conduct summary:

  • Act with integrity.
  • Pursue constant excellence.
  • Respect the hierarchy.
  • Protect company data.

New Hire Checklist

  1. ☐ Sign in to the AfriVate Portal and complete the assigned onboarding checklist and videos.
  2. ☐ Open the SWP under Portal → Resources, read it, and complete its policy acknowledgement.
  3. ☐ Join the AfriVate ClickUp workspace and your assigned team communication spaces.
  4. ☐ Set up your @afrivate email for account access and approved external correspondence.
  5. ☐ Agree 3–5 initial KPIs with your Team Lead and record the goals through Portal → Growth → OKRs.
  6. ☐ Review assigned tasks and submit progress through Portal → Weekly check-in.
