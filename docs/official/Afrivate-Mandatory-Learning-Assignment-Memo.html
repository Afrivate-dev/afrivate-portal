As part of AfriVate’s continuous learning and professional development initiative, every assigned team member is required to complete a structured learning assignment designed to strengthen workplace professionalism and improve the quality of our products and services.

Your Learning Journey

This is more than a completion exercise. It is an opportunity to reflect on how you show up at work, strengthen the habits that build trust, and turn professional knowledge into visible action.

Your goal: Finish with practical actions that make you more reliable, respectful, accountable, and effective in your role.

1. Begin with AfriVate Resources

Before starting the external course, open the Resources section of the AfriVate Portal and carefully review every document made available to you. Complete any required policy acknowledgements.

These resources provide important guidance on:

  • AfriVate’s culture and expectations
  • Design and development standards
  • Recruitment and careers processes
  • Internal workflows and best practices
  • Policies and professional conduct
  • Other materials that support your role

2. Complete Your First Learning Course

Professionalism in the Office

Platform: Alison

This course introduces essential workplace behaviours and professional standards aligned with AfriVate’s commitment to excellence, accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Open the Alison Course Create an Alison Account

Creating Your Alison Account

  1. Visit alison.com.
  2. Select Sign Up.
  3. Register with your preferred email address, preferably the email used for AfriVate communication.
  4. Verify your email address if prompted.
  5. Search for Professionalism in the Office or use the direct course link above.
  6. Complete every course module.

3. Download Your Learner Record

After completing the course, download your Alison Learner Record. This is the required evidence of course completion and allows HR to verify your learning progress.

Watch the Learner Record Tutorial

4. Prepare Your Learning Evaluation

Prepare a concise learning report in one of the following formats:

Document1 to 2 pages
Presentation4 to 5 slides

Your report must address:

  • Workplace Professionalism: Key Lessons: Summarise the most important lessons gained from the AfriVate resources and the Professionalism in the Office course. Focus on professional conduct, communication, accountability, time management, workplace etiquette, collaboration, integrity, and how these behaviours influence the quality of work.
  • Applying Professionalism in Daily Work: Explain how you will apply the course lessons in your current role at AfriVate. Give practical examples covering communication through ClickUp, punctuality, meeting deadlines, accountability, teamwork, handling feedback, resolving disagreements respectfully, protecting company information, and maintaining accurate Portal records.
  • Personal Professional Development Plan: Identify three professional behaviours or habits you will improve over the next 30 days. For each one, state the action you will take, how it will improve your work or team, and how you will measure whether you have made progress.
