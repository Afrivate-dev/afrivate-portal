PORTAL TITLE: AfriVate Slack is now our official work chat. Action this week

AfriVate Slack is now live as our official workspace for day-to-day work conversation.

Use Slack for questions, updates, coordination, clarification, and follow-up. Keep using this Portal for anything that needs a formal record, approval, or acknowledgement. That includes leave, tasks, weekly updates, learning, policy acknowledgements, OKRs, surveys, and people processes.

WhatsApp remains for informal or emergency contact only. It is not for official work instructions.

Remember: Slack is for talking. Portal is for recording. WhatsApp is not for official work.

ACTION REQUIRED THIS WEEK
1. Join Slack and set your real name and profile photo.
2. Turn on notifications for DMs and @mentions.
3. Watch the welcome video and read the House Rules pinned in Slack #announcements.
4. Say hello in your team channel and message your Team Lead.
5. Keep completing leave, tasks, weekly updates, learning, and acknowledgements in this Portal.

AVAILABILITY
On official work days (Monday to Thursday), during your team’s core hours, acknowledge Slack work messages within four (4) hours.

STANDARDS
All AfriVate policies continue to apply on Slack, including the Standard Work Process. Policy acknowledgements stay in Portal > Resources. Slack cannot create or change policy.

For sensitive concerns, use People > Growth > Speak up, or contact People & Culture.

NEED HELP?
If you did not receive a Slack invite, email afrivatehr@gmail.com or message afrivatehr on Slack.

Thank you. Let’s keep work communication clear and in the right place.
