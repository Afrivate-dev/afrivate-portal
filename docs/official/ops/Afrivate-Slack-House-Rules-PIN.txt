AFRIVATE SLACK HOUSE RULES
Pinned in #announcements · Applies to everyone on this workspace

1) WHAT EACH TOOL IS FOR
• Slack = official work chat (questions, updates, coordination, clarification, follow-up)
• Portal (portal.afrivate.org) = official record (leave, tasks, weekly updates, learning, policy acknowledgements, OKRs, surveys, people processes)
• WhatsApp = informal or emergency contact only — not for official work instructions
• Simple rule: Slack talks. Portal records. WhatsApp is not official work.

2) AVAILABILITY
• Official work days: Monday–Thursday (unless your approved team schedule says otherwise)
• In your team’s core hours, acknowledge work messages within 4 hours
• If you will be offline, set a status and hand over anything urgent
• Be reachable on Slack during core hours

3) CHANNELS
• Use the right channel — don’t dump everything in one place
• Keep #announcements for important notices only — discuss in team channels
• @mention the person who must act
• Prefer team channels over long private side chats when the team needs visibility
• Don’t create new public channels without Admin/Lead approval while we settle the workspace

4) CONDUCT
• Be clear, respectful, and professional
• No harassment, insults, discrimination, bullying, or mocking colleagues
• Be honest about progress and blockers — don’t hide problems or invent status
• Disagree on ideas, not on people
• Follow reporting lines; escalate properly (Team Lead → Pillar Head → leadership)
• Sensitive concerns / grievances / safety issues → Portal → People → Growth → Speak up (or People & Culture) — not a public channel fight

5) CONFIDENTIALITY & FILES
• Protect AfriVate, partner, Pathfinder, Enabler, and teammate information
• Don’t share passwords, private people data, or unreleased plans in the wrong place
• Share files carefully — prefer AfriVate Google Drive / approved folders
• Don’t speak for AfriVate externally unless you have prior written authorisation

6) AFRIVATE POLICIES STILL APPLY HERE
• SWP, Leave Policy, Onboarding Handbook, Org Structure, Delegation of Authority, Internal Contributor / Volunteer rules, and Team Lead Playbook all still apply on Slack
• Policy acknowledgements happen in Portal → Resources (a Slack pin does not replace that)
• Slack cannot create, change, or cancel company policy
• Leave, learning, weekly updates, appraisals, and formal people actions must go through Portal
• Breach of these rules / AfriVate policies may lead to coaching or progressive discipline under the SWP

7) DO THIS ONCE YOU JOIN
• Set your real name + profile photo
• Turn on notifications for DMs and @mentions
• Read this pin fully
• Say hello in your team channel and message your Team Lead that you’re on Slack
• Bookmark the Portal: https://portal.afrivate.org

Questions? Ask in the help channel — then complete formal actions in the Portal.

FULL REFERENCE — AfriVate Slack House Rules
These house rules summarise, and do not replace, AfriVate’s official instruments:
• AFRI-SWP — Standard Work Process (how work is done; Slack + Portal system rules; conduct; discipline)
• AFRI-ORG-01 — Organisational Structure (pillars, ownership, Portal Directory controls reporting lines)
• AFRI-DOA-01 — Delegation of Authority (who may decide; escalation; crisis within 60 minutes for leads)
• AFRI-ICEF-01 — Internal Contributor Engagement Framework (unpaid internal contributors; confidentiality; IP; capacity)
• AFRI-VCC — Volunteer Code of Conduct (external volunteers / partner collaborators)
• AFRI-LAP-01 — Leave and Absence Policy (leave only via Portal; People & Culture final approval)
• AFRI-EOH-01 — Team Member Onboarding Handbook
• AFRI-TLOP-01 — Team Lead Operational Playbook
• AFRI-ODR-01 — Official Document Register (precedence; Portal Resources is binding staff-facing copy)
• AFRI-PUG-02 — Portal User Guide for staff (how-to)
• AFRI-PUG-03 — Portal User Guide for team leads (how-to)
• AFRI-PUG-01 — Portal User Guide full / Admin (how-to)

Hard lines:
• A Slack message never creates a Portal approval or policy change
• Titles/authority claims on Slack that contradict Portal Directory are void
• Formal people actions (leave, learning proof, weekly update, acknowledgements, appraisals) = Portal
• Respect, honesty, confidentiality, and professionalism are mandatory everywhere we work — including Slack
