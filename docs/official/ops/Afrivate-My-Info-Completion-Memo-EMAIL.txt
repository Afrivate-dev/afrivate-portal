Team,

This is a short but important request from Human Resources.

We need every team member to complete their personal information on the AfriVate Portal so HR has accurate, up to date details on file. This is time sensitive. Please treat it as a priority and complete it as soon as you can, without delay.

WHAT TO DO
1. Go to https://portal.afrivate.org and sign in.
2. In the sidebar, open People.
3. Click My Info.
4. Fill in every field completely and accurately.
5. Click Save my info.

WHAT TO COMPLETE
Please make sure the following are filled:
• Preferred name and legal name
• Personal email and phone number
• Work location and country
• Date of birth
• Pronouns (if you use them)
• LinkedIn URL, bio, and skills
• Emergency contact name, phone, and relationship
• Next of kin notes (where relevant)

Aim for full profile completeness. Do not leave blank fields that apply to you.

WHY THIS MATTERS
HR needs complete records to support the team properly, reach you when it matters, and keep emergency contacts ready. Incomplete profiles create delays and risk for everyone.

TIMING
This request is time sensitive. Please complete My Info promptly. Do not wait until later in the week if you can do it now.

NEED HELP?
If you cannot access the Portal, or something on My Info will not save, email afrivatehr@gmail.com or message afrivatehr on Slack.
