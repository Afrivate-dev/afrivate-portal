SUBJECT: AfriVate Slack is live. Action needed this week

MEMO: AFRI-MEMO-SLACK-01
TO: All AfriVate Team Members
FROM: People & Culture / Human Resources Manager
CC: Chief Executive Officer
DATE: 11 August 2026
RE: Official adoption of AfriVate Slack for day-to-day work communication
STATUS: Action required

Team,

AfriVate has launched our official Slack workspace for day-to-day work conversation. This memo confirms the change, what we expect from you, and what you should do this week.

Slack is now the approved place for questions, updates, coordination, clarification, and follow-up during work. It does not replace the AfriVate Portal.

WHAT EACH TOOL IS FOR
• Slack: official work chat
• Portal (https://portal.afrivate.org): official record for leave, tasks, weekly updates, learning, policy acknowledgements, OKRs, surveys, and people processes
• WhatsApp: informal or emergency contact only. Not for official work instructions, approvals, or policy matters

Remember: Slack is for talking. Portal is for recording. WhatsApp is not for official work.

WHAT YOU MUST DO THIS WEEK
1. Join or open the AfriVate Slack workspace using your invite.
2. Set your real name and profile photo.
3. Turn on notifications for direct messages and @mentions.
4. Watch the welcome video pinned in #announcements.
5. Read the Slack House Rules pinned in #announcements.
6. Say hello in your team channel and message your Team Lead that you are on Slack.
7. Keep using the Portal for leave, tasks, weekly updates, learning, and policy acknowledgements.

AVAILABILITY
Official work days remain Monday to Thursday (unless your approved team schedule says otherwise). During your team’s core hours, acknowledge work messages on Slack within four (4) hours.

CONDUCT
AfriVate standards still apply on Slack: professionalism, respect, honesty about progress and blockers, confidentiality, and proper escalation. Sensitive concerns go through Portal > People > Growth > Speak up, or contact People & Culture. Please do not turn sensitive issues into a public channel argument.

Slack cannot create, change, or cancel company policy. Official policies and acknowledgements remain in Portal > Resources.

SUPPORT
If you have not received a Slack invite, or you cannot access the workspace, email afrivatehr@gmail.com or message afrivatehr on Slack once you are in.

Thank you for helping us keep communication clear, professional, and in the right place.

Emmanuel Okpiaifo,
