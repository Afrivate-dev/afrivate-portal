This guide is only for team leads and assistant leads. It covers what you can see and do in the portal — your own work and the extra tools for the people you manage.

It does not cover Admin, recruitment, appraisals, discipline paperwork, or role changes. You will not see those. People & Culture use the full AFRI-PUG-01. Ordinary staff use AFRI-PUG-02.

1. Who this is for

Role on your profileWhat this means in the portal
Assistant leadYou do everything a team member does, plus approve leave for your people, see their weekly updates, see a team snapshot, mark 1:1s, review their IDPs, see team OKRs, see PIP/suspension badges, and open management-only files. You cannot publish memos or add calendar events.
Team leadEverything an assistant lead can do, plus publish memos and add calendar events.

You still do not see Admin in the menu. That is correct. You cannot approve new joiners, change roles, open recruitment, write appraisals, or place someone on a PIP. Send those to People & Culture.

If nobody “belongs” to you yet (no reports-to, no team, no department head role), team tabs will look empty. Ask HR to set reporting lines.

2. The golden rule

If it needs a record, it belongs in the Portal. Slack is for chat (acknowledge official Slack messages within four (4) hours on official work days). WhatsApp is informal or emergency only. A Slack “please approve my leave” is not an approval. Click Approve in the Portal.

5. Home (your extra numbers)

Path: Home

Same banners as staff (surveys, learning, policies), plus a count of leave waiting on you (your people’s pending requests, and your own). Start here. Clear your own banners. Then open Time off if the leave number is not zero.

6. Your own work (you still do all of this)

You are not exempt from staff habits.

  • My work — create tasks, assign people, mark Complete (the portal stores who and when). Views: Board, List, Week.
  • Inbox — your alerts (leave comments, shout-outs, assignments, memos).
  • Search — people, memos, tasks, documents you may open (including management-only), events, leave in your scope. Not HR-only files or recruitment.
  • Weekly update — submit your four fields, then open the Team tab (section 8).
  • Notes, shout-outs, learning, surveys, My info — same as staff. Keep your own My info complete.
  • Directory — edit your card. You cannot edit other people’s profiles. You can see PIP / suspension badges on others.

7. Approve leave (the most important extra)

Path: People → Time off

Staff only see My requests. You also see Team requests and Calendar. Leave is official only in the Portal. Do not “approve” on WhatsApp or Slack and skip the button here.

  1. Open People → Time off → Team requests.
  2. Open the request. Check dates, type (annual / sick / emergency), reason, and any attachment.
  3. Confirm Approve or Decline, and add a short note. The person is notified in their Inbox.
  4. Use Calendar to see who is off that month.

You see people linked to you through reports-to, your team, or a department you head. You do not see the whole organisation. Except emergencies, expect at least three (3) official work days’ notice and a work handover (they should also tell the team on Slack). Until you click Approve, they are not on approved leave. You cannot delete a leave request. People & Culture can. Typical allowances shown: annual 20 days, sick 10, emergency 7.

8. Team weekly updates

Path: Weekly update → Team

See who on your team has submitted this week and who has not. Open a person’s update to read finished / next / blockers / hours. You can filter by department. Chase missing updates on Slack if you like — but the record is this page. HR can also see org-wide check-ins; you cannot.

9. People overview — team snapshot and duty status

Path: People (Overview)

My team snapshot shows combined numbers for people you manage (headcount, engagement, eNPS, and similar). You do not see anyone’s individual survey answers. If no numbers appear, reporting lines may not be set. Ask HR.

PIP & suspension (visible to leads, HR, and admin) lists people currently on a Performance Improvement Plan or suspension. You will also see these badges on Directory profiles. You cannot change duty status.

StatusWhat it meansWhat you can do
On PIPThey keep full portal access. A banner shows for them.Support them. You cannot create or close the formal PIP file. That is HR.
SuspendedThey can sign in and read Memos and Resources only.Do not expect tasks, leave, or check-ins from them until HR lifts it.

10. Growth as a manager

Path: People → Growth

You still have your own OKRs, IDP, feedback, milestones, awards, and Speak up. On top of that:

OKRs

Your own objectives for the quarter, with key-result sliders. Below that, Team OKRs (read-only) — you can see your people’s aims and progress. You cannot edit theirs.

1:1s

Meet outside the portal (Google Meet, Slack huddle, in person). Then come here and Mark done for each person this month. That is how HR knows the conversation happened. You will also see whether your manager has marked your 1:1 complete. You cannot mark your own.

IDP

Write and submit your plan. If people who report to you have submitted theirs, an IDP reviews panel appears. Read the plan, write manager feedback, click Mark reviewed. They will see your note.

Feedback

When HR opens a 360° cycle, complete the forms assigned to you (including as a manager). You cannot open or close a cycle. HR does that.

Speak up

Your own confidential concerns go to HR. You do not receive other people’s Speak up submissions.

11. Publish memos (team lead only)

Path: Memos

Assistant leads: skip this section. You can read memos; you cannot publish.

Team leads: compose a memo (title, body or attached document, category, priority: Info / Important / Urgent). Choose audience: everyone, or a department. Attach images or files if needed. Drafts can save on your device until you publish. You can edit memos you posted. You cannot delete other people’s memos — HR/Admin can. If you need an organisation-wide HR digest, ask People & Culture.

12. Add calendar events (team lead only)

Path: Calendar

Assistant leads: skip this section. You can browse events; you cannot add them.

Team leads: create an event (title, date, start/end time, location, audience: everyone or a department). Drafts can save locally before you publish. Staff will see it on Home (if it is soon) and on Calendar.

13. Resources (including management-only)

Path: Resources

Browse, preview, download, and acknowledge policies that require it — same as staff. Extra: files marked management only are visible to you. Staff cannot see them. Files marked HR only are still hidden from you. You cannot upload or delete library files. People & Culture manage the library.

14. AVA

Same helper as staff. Ask “How do I approve leave?” and use Go to Time off. She never clicks Approve for you. She will not show you HR records you are not allowed to see.

17. A good weekly habit for leads

  1. Home — your banners first, then any leave waiting on you.
  2. Time off → Team requests — do not leave people pending.
  3. Weekly update — submit yours, then check Team for gaps.
  4. Growth → 1:1s — if you met someone this month, mark it done.
  5. Keep your My info, learning, and surveys current — you model the behaviour.
  6. Records in the Portal. Chat on Slack. Escalate to hr@afrivate.org when it is an HR decision.
