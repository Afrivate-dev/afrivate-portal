This is the full how-to guide for AfriVate Team Space (all roles, including Admin). It is written in plain English.

Role-specific guides: Staff / ordinary team members use AFRI-PUG-02 (Staff Portal Guide). Team leads and assistant leads use AFRI-PUG-03 (Team Lead Portal Guide). People & Culture and Administrators use this document (AFRI-PUG-01).
In one minute: Team Space is the official work website. If something needs a record (leave, a weekly update, a learning certificate, a policy you have read, an appraisal), it belongs here. If something is just a chat, use Slack. WhatsApp is only for informal or emergency contact. AVA can show you the right page, but she never submits anything for you.

1. The golden rule: which tool to use

Portal (Team Space)Anything that must be recorded: leave, weekly updates, tasks, learning proof, surveys, shout-outs, growth plans, memos, appraisals, discipline, PIPs, your personal details, and signing that you have read a policy.Casual chat.

AlisonTaking the assigned online course.Proving you finished it — that proof goes in the Portal.

Remember: If it needs a stamp of “this happened, and here is the proof,” use the Portal.

2. Who can do what

Everyone sees a role on their profile. That role decides what extra buttons you get.

Role you seeWhat you can do
Team memberEveryday work: tasks, leave requests, weekly updates, learning, surveys, shout-outs, My info, growth tools, notes, memos (read), resources, calendar.
Assistant leadEverything a team member can do, plus approve leave for the people you manage, and see their weekly updates.
Team leadEverything an assistant lead can do, plus publish memos and add calendar events.
People & culture (HR)Everything above, plus the Admin area: approvals, leave for the whole organisation, the HR dashboard, Recruitment, and the Employee hub.
AdministratorEverything HR can do, plus change someone’s role, and manage departments and teams.

Leads usually see the people who report to them, the people on their team, or the people in a department they head. Only Administrators can change a person’s role.

3. Getting into the portal

Website: portal.afrivate.org

Request access (first time)

You cannot just walk in. Someone from People & Culture (or an Administrator) must approve you.

  1. On the login screen, click Request access.
  2. Enter your full name, work email, and a password.
  3. Choose your department and type your job title. You may add a short message.
  4. Click submit.

Password rules: at least 8 characters, with one uppercase letter, and one number or symbol. After you submit, you wait. You may be able to sign in, but you will see a “waiting for approval” screen until HR or Admin clicks Approve under Admin → Approvals.

Sign in

  1. Go to the portal and open Sign in.
  2. Enter your email and password, then click sign in.
  3. Or use a magic link: enter your email, ask for a link, open the email, and click it. No password needed that time.
  4. If your account is not approved yet, you will see a waiting screen.

Forgot your password

  1. On the login screen, click Forgot password.
  2. Enter your email, open the message you receive, and click the link.
  3. Set a new password (same rules as above) and sign in again.

Change email or password later

  1. Click your name or photo in the top-right corner.
  2. Open Account & security.
  3. Change your email (you will confirm via email) or set a new password.

You can read the privacy notice at any time from Account or /privacy. To leave, click your name → Sign out and confirm.

5. Home

Path: Home (the first page after you sign in)

Home is your “good morning” page. It greets you by name and shows what needs attention.

What you will see

  • Shortcut buttons: New task, Request leave, Submit check-in.
  • Numbers for: tasks due today, leave waiting (yours, or yours to approve if you are a lead), unread memos, and events coming in the next 7 days.
  • Coloured reminder banners if you have an open survey, a learning certificate due, or a policy you have not yet acknowledged.
  • If you joined in the last 30 days: a Getting started progress bar for onboarding videos.
  • Today’s events and the latest memos (open one to read it and mark it as read).

What to do: Start here each work day. Tap any banner or card — it takes you to the right page. Clear banners by actually doing the thing (submit the survey, upload the certificate, acknowledge the policy).

6. My work (tasks)

Path: My work

This is the shared to-do list. You can create work, assign it to people, track status, and mark it complete. When someone marks a task complete, the portal stores who did it and when.

Views: Board (columns: Up next → In progress → Complete / Stuck), List (grouped by due date), and Week (laid out across the current week).

Filters: Mine / Owned / Assigned; status; priority (urgent, normal, later); category; and search by title.

How to create a task

  1. Click New task (from Home or My work).
  2. Fill in a title (required). Add a description if it helps.
  3. Choose assignees, a due date, priority, category, estimated hours, hours already spent, and any blockers.
  4. Save.

How to update a task

  1. Open the task. Change status, add comments, update hours or blockers.
  2. Move it: Up next → In progress → Complete. If you are stuck, mark it Stuck and write what is blocking you.
  3. When the work is finished, mark Complete. Do not just tell someone on Slack — the Portal timestamp is the official record.

7. Inbox

Path: Inbox (or the bell in the top bar)

Inbox is not email. It is a list of portal alerts for you: shout-outs and comments, tasks assigned to you or mentions, leave approved/declined or a comment on your request, survey reminders, a new memo, access approved or denied, or a department change.

  1. Open Inbox.
  2. Tap an item — it is marked as read and you jump to the related page.
  3. Use Mark all as read if you want the red dot gone.

8. Search

Path: Search (or the search icon in the top bar)

Type a person’s name, a memo title, a task, a document name, or any keyword. Results can include people, memos, tasks, notes, documents, events, leave, shout-outs, surveys, learning, OKRs, and (for HR) recruitment and related records. You only see what your role is allowed to see.

9. Weekly update

Path: Weekly update

Every week you tell the team four simple things: what you finished, what you will do next, anything blocking you, and how many hours you worked. You can choose who sees it: your department, or everyone.

  1. Open Weekly update. Stay on This week.
  2. Fill in the four fields, choose visibility, and submit. You can come back and edit the same week later.
  3. Open History to see your past weeks.

Leads open the Team tab to see who has submitted. HR can also review the whole organisation under Admin → Check-ins. A Slack “I was busy this week” message is not a substitute.

10. Getting started (onboarding)

Path: Getting started

This page is mainly for people who just joined.

  • Videos: Watch the welcome videos in order. Mark them watched as you go. Home shows your progress for the first 30 days.
  • Checklist: Tick first-week items as you complete them (some items may tick themselves when you do the related Portal action).

People & Culture add and edit videos and checklist items under Admin → Onboarding.

11. Notes

Path: Notes

Notes are shared pages — meeting notes, plans, how-we-do-things write-ups. They can nest under other pages, like folders.

  1. Create a page and write in the editor.
  2. Choose who can see it: Private, Workspace (everyone), Departments or Teams, or named People.
  3. You can share a link when link sharing is turned on.
  4. Delete only when you are sure — it is removed for everyone who had access.

12. Memos

Path: Memos

Memos are official written updates from the company or a department. Some HR digests here are the same content HR also sends by email. Each memo has a title, body (or an attached document that is the body), a category, a priority (Info, Important, or Urgent), an audience, and optional pictures or files.

How to read: Open Memos, filter or search if needed, read the memo, and mark it as read. Home will keep reminding you about unread ones.

Who can publish: Team lead and above can write and publish memos. Drafts can save on your own device until you are ready. Set the audience (everyone or a department) and priority. HR / Admin can edit or delete memos. HR can draft an HR digest from the HR dashboard.

If your account is suspended, Memos is one of the two areas you can still read (the other is Resources).

13. Resources (policies and files)

Path: Resources

This is the filing cabinet: handbooks, policies, templates, and similar files. Some files are open to everyone. Some are HR only. Some are management only. Some require you to click that you have read them (acknowledge).

  1. Browse by category, or search by name.
  2. Open a file to preview it, or download it.
  3. If the file needs acknowledgement, complete that action. Home and People will keep a yellow banner until you do.

HR can upload files (from the computer or Google Drive), set the category, and tick Requires acknowledgement, HR only, or Management only. HR can also see who has acknowledged a policy from the HR dashboard. If your account is suspended, you can still read Resources.

14. Calendar

Path: Calendar

The team calendar of events. Views: list, schedule (month / week / day), and a Google Calendar view if one is connected. Browse upcoming events and click one for time, place, and who it is for. Events may be for everyone or for a department. Team lead and above can create events (title, date, start/end time, location, audience). Drafts can save locally before you publish.

15. People hub

Path: People

People is one place for “anything about humans at AfriVate” that is not Admin. Tabs: Overview · Time off · Shout-outs · Learning · Surveys · Growth · My info · Directory.

Overview has shortcuts, the same reminder banners as Home, recent HR digest memos, a team snapshot for leads (combined numbers only — individual survey answers stay with HR), and a PIP & suspension list for leads, HR, and admin.

16. Time off (leave)

Path: People → Time off

This is the only official way to request leave. A WhatsApp message, a Slack ping, a phone call, or a verbal “I’m off tomorrow” is not an approved request (except a true emergency, which you still record here as soon as you can).

TypeEveryday meaningTypical allowance shown
AnnualPlanned holiday / vacation20 days
SickYou are unwell10 days
EmergencySudden, unavoidable absence7 days

Except accepted emergencies, give at least three (3) official work days’ notice. Finish or hand over your work, and tell colleagues on Slack, before you go.

How to request leave

  1. Open People → Time off (or Home → Request leave, or ask AVA → Go to Time off).
  2. Click Request leave.
  3. Choose start date, end date, type (annual / sick / emergency), and a reason.
  4. Attach a supporting file if needed (for example a medical note). You can upload a file or pick one from Google Drive.
  5. Confirm, then submit. Watch status under My requests: pending → approved or declined.
  6. You and your manager can leave comments on the request.

How leads approve: Open People → Time off → Team requests (or All requests if you are HR). Read the dates, reason, and any attachment. Approve or Decline with a short note. Use the Calendar tab to see who is off. HR can also approve from Admin → Leave. Until the Portal says approved, you are not on approved leave.

17. Shout-outs

Path: People → Shout-outs

This is the praise wall. Thank a colleague in public and tag it with a value. People & Culture manage the tag list.

  1. Compose a shout-out. Choose the person, write a short message, pick a tag, and add a photo if you like.
  2. Send. It appears on the feed. Others can like and comment.

HR can remove a shout-out if it should not stay on the feed.

18. Learning (Alison courses)

Path: People → Learning

AfriVate assigns courses on Alison (an external learning website). You take the course there. You prove you finished it here.

  1. Open People → Learning and open the assigned Alison course.
  2. Finish the course on Alison and download or photograph your certificate.
  3. Back in the Portal, enter the course name, the completion date, and upload the certificate (or pick it from Google Drive).
  4. Submit. Status is pending until HR reviews it. If HR rejects it, fix what they asked for and resubmit.

Home and People will show a banner until your proof is in. HR publishes assignments and reviews certificates from this page and from the HR dashboard.

19. Surveys

Path: People → Surveys

From time to time HR opens a short survey: Pulse (how are you finding work), eNPS (would you recommend AfriVate as a place to work), or Onboarding. One response per person. Your individual answers go to HR. Leads may see combined team scores, not your personal answers.

  1. Open People → Surveys, or tap the Home / People banner.
  2. Open the active survey, answer the questions, and submit.

HR launches surveys, sends reminders, and reads results under Admin → HR dashboard.

21. My info

Path: People → My info

This is your personal record for HR. Keep it accurate. Incomplete records create extra chasing from People & Culture.

You can update: preferred name and legal name, personal email and phone, work location and country, date of birth, pronouns, LinkedIn, bio and skills, emergency contact (name, phone, relationship), and next-of-kin notes.

You cannot change yourself: employment status, engagement type (employee / volunteer / contractor), probation dates, payroll flags, and other HR-only fields. Those live under Admin → Employees.

Fill in the form, check the profile completeness percentage, and click Save my info. If HR has asked you to review your details, you will see an amber banner until you save an update.

22. Directory

Path: People → Directory (or My profile in the top-right menu)

The staff list. Search by name. Open a person to see job title, department, contact details, availability, and (if you are allowed) PIP or suspension status. Edit your own profile card (photo, bio, phone, skills, LinkedIn). HR / Admin can edit other people’s cards and assign department or team. Availability (Available / Away / Busy / Focusing) comes from the top-bar control.

23. AVA — the in-portal helper

Where: Purple AVA button (bottom-right on a computer; above the phone menu)

AVA means AfriVate Virtual Assistant. She is a help desk inside the portal — not a manager, and not a robot that does your work.

She can: answer how-to questions; explain the four-hour Slack rule and which tool to use; show a Go to… button that opens the right page; and use a snapshot of your open tasks, leave, and surveys so the answer fits you.

She cannot: submit, approve, reject, edit, or delete anything; fill in a leave form or weekly update and save it for you; replace a People & Culture decision; see records you are not allowed to see; or give salary, legal, or confidential HR advice. For those, email hr@afrivate.org.

  1. Click AVA.
  2. Type a question, or tap a suggested prompt (for example “How do I request leave?”).
  3. Read the steps. If a Go to… button appears, click it.
  4. Complete the action yourself on that page.
  5. Close AVA with the close button or the Escape key.

If AVA is down, use this User Guide under Resources, or email hr@afrivate.org.

25. Admin — for People & Culture and Administrators

Path: Admin — HR and Admin roles only

Admin is the back office. Staff do not see it. Tabs: Approvals · Recruitment · Users · Departments · Teams · Announcements · Leave · Onboarding · Check-ins · HR dashboard · Employees. Departments and Teams are Administrator only.

Approvals

People who requested access wait here. Open the request, check name, email, department, and job title. Set the right role if needed. Click Approve (they can use the portal) or Deny (they stay out and can request again later). You can also invite someone by email if they have not requested yet.

Users

The list of portal accounts. Search people. Edit department, job title, who they report to, and whether the account is active. Set duty status (normal / on PIP / suspended) — you will be asked to confirm. Administrators only can change role. You cannot deactivate your own signed-in account. Deactivating someone means they can no longer sign in. That is different from suspension, where they can still sign in with a locked-down view.

Departments (Administrator only)

Create, rename, or delete departments. Optionally assign a department head. Assigning a person to a department can update their reporting line to that head.

Teams (Administrator only)

Create teams with a lead, optional assistant lead, and a link to a department. Add or remove members. A person’s department may align with the team’s department when you add them.

Announcements

Moderate organisation-wide memos from Admin: create, edit, delete, set audience and priority. Same content as the Memos page, from the HR/Admin side.

Leave

The organisation-wide leave queue and calendar. Approve or decline pending requests, add a note, and (when authorised) delete a request.

Onboarding

Add, edit, or remove welcome videos (YouTube links, section, duration) and checklist items that new joiners see under Getting started.

Check-ins

A digest of everyone’s weekly updates. Filter by week or department. This is how HR sees who submitted and what they wrote.

HR dashboard

The people-operations overview. Numbers typically include headcount, engagement, eNPS, learning completion, 1:1 rate, leave, grievances, surveys, attrition, time-to-hire, policy acknowledgements, OKRs, recognition, onboarding CSAT, active PIPs, pending discipline, probation due, and people on suspension.

From this dashboard you can also: export KPIs; assign Alison courses and review certificates; launch pulse / eNPS surveys, manage templates, and remind non-responders; manage awards, IDP review, policy acknowledgements, exit interviews, and grievances; open 360° feedback cycles, assign reviewers, and manage templates; draft an HR digest memo; adjust portal labels (categories and tags); and open the Revival launch checklist if you are on the allowlist.

26. Employee hub (HR records)

Path: Admin → Employees

This is the system of record for each person’s employment file. Tabs: Directory · Discipline & PIP · Appraisals · Probation · Lead scorecards · Offboarding · Audit.

Directory and person dossier

Search active people by engagement type, employment status, and how complete their My info is. Open a dossier (the full file) for one person. On a dossier you can:

  • Set duty status: Normal duty, On PIP, or Suspended (with a confirmation).
  • Set engagement (employee / volunteer / contractor) and status (active, probation, on leave, exiting, terminated, archived).
  • Record start date, probation end, confirmation date, payroll setup, contract or volunteer terms.
  • Keep HR-private notes (these stay off the default PDF).
  • Request employee to update My Info — they see a banner on My info.
  • Review discipline history and open a new case.
  • Export PDF (optionally include discipline) and Archive when appropriate.

Discipline & PIP

AfriVate uses a stepped process: (1) Coaching / verbal, (2) Written warning, (3) Performance Improvement Plan (PIP), (4) Restricted duties, (5) Termination case.

When you record a case, capture severity (low, medium, high, critical), the trigger (for example missed deadlines, communication, absence, misconduct, underperformance), and how it was delivered (portal notice, meeting, formal email, written letter). You can Activate (HR) immediately, or save a pending recommendation for HR to approve. Termination stays carefully gated.

For an active PIP: track goals and review cadence, mark whether the person is on track, and close with passed / extended / escalated / terminated recommendation. Creating a PIP step can auto-create a PIP with templated goals and duration. Setting duty status to “On PIP” on the dossier is the visible badge; the Discipline tab is the formal paperwork. Close both when the plan ends.

Appraisals

Formal appraisals score two parts: 60% output / deliverables and 40% soft skills / behaviour. Those two scores become one overall percentage and a band:

Overall scoreBand
70% or moreExceptional
60–69%Good
50–59%Performance concern — coaching
40–49%Disciplinary / corrective
Below 40%Termination consideration

Usual cadence: quarterly, or monthly while someone is on an active PIP. Save drafts, finalise, and keep history on the person’s record. Use the branded Performance Appraisal Form as a printable worksheet if you need paper.

Probation

See who is approaching a probation end date. Confirm them into active status and store the confirmation details.

Lead scorecards

Track quality signals for leads — for example delivery consistency, KPI completion, communication discipline, and escalation quality.

Offboarding

When someone is leaving, start a checklist and pick a reason. Work through the default items: finish or reassign Portal tasks, final weekly update, knowledge transfer, revoke access (email, Slack, Portal), collect assets, exit interview notes, and volunteer Code of Conduct notices when relevant. Tick items as done.

Audit

A chronological log of HR actions on profiles, discipline, PIPs, appraisals, and offboarding. Use it when you need to see who changed what, and when.

27. Recruitment

Path: Admin → Recruitment

This is the applicant tracker for open roles. It is for People & Culture (and Admin), not for general staff. You can define scoring criteria for a role, connect Gmail (the HR mailbox) so applications can sync in, search candidates, preview CVs, read the application email, rank / rescore candidates, and move people through stages: Applied → Screen → Interview → Offer → Hired or Rejected. When the portal asks you to connect Gmail, complete the Google sign-in popup. Treat applications as confidential.

28. Special: Revival launch checklist

Only named launch owners can open this. It is a shared playbook for a specific launch (portal readiness, memos, WhatsApp drafts, Alison, surveys, town halls, and similar). Most staff will never see this page.

30. A good weekly habit

  1. Open Home. Clear the banners (survey, learning, policy).
  2. Put leave and any formal People & Culture matter in the Portal — not WhatsApp or Slack.
  3. Keep My work honest. Mark tasks Complete so the time and the person are recorded.
  4. Submit the Weekly update before the week closes.
  5. Keep My info complete so HR does not have to chase you.
  6. Use Slack for chat. Use the Portal whenever something must be on the record.
  7. If you are lost, ask AVA, then do the click yourself on the page she opens.
