We are not evaluating how much content you can produce. We are evaluating thinking, creativity, strategic judgement, understanding of digital audiences, and the ability to turn ideas into effective content.

1. About AfriVate

AfriVate Technologies Ltd builds platforms and programmes that connect African Pathfinders (talent) with Enablers (organisations) through volunteering, internships, mentorship, micro-tasks, remote work, and related opportunities — elevating life and professional growth across Africa.

Our mission is practical: create technology and operating systems that improve how African talent is discovered, developed, and connected to opportunity. Our audience is largely young, digitally engaged, ambitious, and proud of African identity and progress.

Brand voice

  • Innovation · Elevation · Technology — the AfriVate tagline.
  • Excellence: Work meets stated success criteria. Completion without quality is not success.
  • Ownership: Outcomes, communication, and accurate reporting — not only effort.
  • Professionalism: Clear, respectful, and specific. Avoid hype that AfriVate cannot stand behind.

Visual identity is purple-led (#8d4087), clean, and modern. Content should feel African, ambitious, and useful — not generic “tech startup” filler.

3. Part 1 — Social media strategy

Using the brief above, develop a short social media strategy for AfriVate that includes:

  • 3–4 content pillars
  • The target audience for each pillar (or overall, if shared)
  • The purpose of each content pillar (why it exists, what it achieves)
  • Recommended platforms and why they fit AfriVate’s audience
  • What you believe AfriVate should be known for online

This section should demonstrate that you can think beyond “posting content”. We want strategic reasoning.

4. Part 2 — 4-day content calendar

Build a 4-day content calendar for AfriVate. Official AfriVate work days are Monday to Thursday; you may plan for those four days or for a typical social calendar — say which you chose. For each day, include:

  • Date
  • Platform
  • Content format (for example Reel, carousel, static post, story)
  • Topic
  • Hook
  • Caption / concept
  • Objective (for example awareness, engagement, education, conversion)
  • Call to action (CTA)

A table format is preferred. This section shows whether you can translate strategy into day-to-day execution.

5. Part 3 — Create actual content

Please create the following two pieces of content. We are evaluating concept and thinking, not design polish. Wireframe-level mockups, annotated sketches, or simple slide layouts are acceptable — do not spend extra time making these pixel-perfect.

5.1 Instagram / LinkedIn carousel

Topic: “5 Ways Technology Can Improve Everyday Life in Africa”

Sketch the carousel slides (low-fidelity mockups, sketches, or a simple slide layout) together with the caption you would post alongside it.

5.2 Short-form video concept

Create a 30–60 second Reel / TikTok / Short concept explaining AfriVate or one of its core values (Innovation, Elevation, Technology — or Excellence, Ownership, Professionalism).

You do not need to film yourself. You may submit either:

  • A full concept including script, shot-by-shot direction, and caption, or
  • A finished video, if you prefer to produce one

6. Part 4 — Copywriting

AfriVate is making the following announcement:

Sample announcement: “AfriVate is opening a new Pathfinder intake — volunteering, internship, mentorship, and remote-work opportunities with Enablers building tech solutions to everyday problems across Africa.”

Based on this announcement, write:

  • A LinkedIn caption
  • An Instagram caption
  • A strong call to action (CTA)

We are looking for evidence that you understand different platforms require different tones and structures.

7. Part 5 — Analytics and decision-making

Below is a small (fictional) dataset showing the performance of four recent AfriVate posts:

Based on this data, please answer:

  • Which content would you produce more of, and why?
  • Which would you change or discontinue, and why?
  • What do these numbers suggest about the audience?
  • What would you test over the next 30 days to improve performance?

8. Part 6 — Community building

AfriVate wants to build a community around young Africans interested in technology, innovation, digital careers, and solutions to African challenges.

Propose three practical ways you would grow and engage this community over the next 30 days.

This helps us distinguish someone who can actually manage a community from someone who simply knows the terminology.
