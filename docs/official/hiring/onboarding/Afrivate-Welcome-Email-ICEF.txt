This email is your Day 0 / Day 1 map. Please read it with your Internal Contributor Engagement Letter. That letter, together with AFRI-ICEF-01, is the written record of your placement. Portal access and Slack do not create employment or any right to pay.

WHAT EACH TOOL IS FOR
• Slack — official work chat (questions, updates, coordination)
• Portal (https://portal.afrivate.org) — official record (tasks, weekly updates, leave, learning, policy acknowledgements, OKRs)
• WhatsApp — informal or emergency contact only. Not for official work instructions.

Slack talks. Portal records. WhatsApp is not official work.

YOUR FIRST OFFICIAL WORK DAYS (Monday–Thursday)

Day 1
1. Sign in at https://portal.afrivate.org
2. Open People → My info and complete your photo, phone, and emergency contact
3. Check People → Directory: department, job title, and reports-to must be correct. If anything is wrong, email hr@afrivate.org the same day
4. Open Getting started — watch the welcome videos and work through the checklist
5. Join Slack with the invite we sent. Set your real name and a professional photo. Turn on notifications for DMs and @mentions
6. Read the Slack House Rules pin in #announcements
7. Say hello in your team channel and message your Team Lead, [Team Lead name], that you are on Slack

Day 2
• Open Resources and acknowledge the required documents (Standard Work Process, Organisational Structure, Internal Contributor Engagement Framework, Leave and Absence Policy, and the Onboarding Handbook)
• Review tasks in My work and ask your Team Lead to clarify anything that is unclear

Days 3–4
• Agree your Agreed Capacity and 3–5 KPIs with your Team Lead
• Record those KPIs in People → Growth → OKRs
• Start the work. Update the Portal task every official work day you touch it

Days 5–7
• Submit your first Weekly update even if progress was limited (explain why)
• Complete any learning assignment in People → Learning

AVAILABILITY
Official work days are Monday to Thursday unless your approved schedule says otherwise. During your team’s core hours, acknowledge work messages on Slack within four (4) hours.

NEED HELP?
• Tasks and day-to-day work — your Team Lead on Slack
• Access, policies, leave, learning — hr@afrivate.org or afrivatehr@gmail.com
• Conduct or safety concerns — Portal → People → Growth → Speak up

We are glad you are here. Do the Portal steps first; everything else is easier after that.
