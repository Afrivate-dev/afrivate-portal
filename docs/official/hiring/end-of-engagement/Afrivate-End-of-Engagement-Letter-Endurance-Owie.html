Status: Portal and Slack access are revoked with immediate effect. This letter does not create employment or any right to pay.

Dear Endurance,

This letter serves as formal notice that your engagement as an unpaid Internal Contributor with AfriVate Technologies Ltd, under the AfriVate Internal Contributor Engagement Framework (AFRI-ICEF-01), is being brought to an end with immediate effect.

This decision follows your continued unresponsiveness on official AfriVate communication channels — including Slack and the AfriVate Portal — for a period exceeding two (2) weeks. Despite reasonable attempts by your Team Lead and People & Culture to reach you, no update, acknowledgement, or explanation was received within this period. This conduct constitutes a breach of the following AfriVate policies:

  • AFRI-ICEF-01 (Internal Contributor Engagement Framework), §5 – Contributor Obligations, which requires you to meet commitments, keep Portal records accurate, and acknowledge official Slack messages within four (4) hours on official work days during core hours;
  • AFRI-SWP (Standard Work Process), §6.1 – the four-hour Slack acknowledgement rule, §7.2 – keeping Portal tasks and Weekly check-ins current, and §12 – Discipline and Corrective Action, under which prolonged, unexplained disengagement is treated as a conduct trigger;
  • AFRI-PDP-01 (Penalty & Disciplinary Policy), §6.1 – Attendance, Availability, and Leave, which classifies prolonged, unexplained disengagement from Portal and Slack activity during an active Agreed Capacity period as a Category C offense warranting suspension review and, on continuation, end of engagement; and
  • AFRI-EOH-01 (Team Member Onboarding Handbook), §5 – Communication Standards, and §7 – Performance, KPIs, and Reporting, which require timely Portal updates and Weekly check-ins even where progress is limited.

Please note that your confidentiality obligations and the assignment of intellectual property in any Work Product under AFRI-ICEF-01 §5.4 and §6 survive the end of this engagement. You remain prohibited from disclosing any AfriVate Confidential Information to a third party without prior written authorisation from AfriVate. A breach of this obligation may result in AfriVate taking appropriate legal action against you.

As this engagement was unpaid internal contribution and did not constitute a contract of employment, no severance, notice pay, salary, stipend, or other financial entitlement arises from this end of engagement, consistent with AFRI-ICEF-01's binding status disclaimer.

AfriVate appreciates your prior contributions and wishes you success in your future endeavours.

Yours faithfully,
