1. Weekly Overview

The week of 10–14 August 2026 focused primarily on strengthening AfriVate's internal communication structure, improving employee access to work systems, reinforcing organizational compliance, strengthening team leadership, and addressing current staffing needs through recruitment.

Significant progress was made in transitioning AfriVate from WhatsApp to Slack as its official workplace communication platform, onboarding employees onto the AfriVate portal, establishing new pseudo team leadership positions, and reopening recruitment for key roles.

2. Key Achievements

A. Internal Communication & Collaboration

Official Transition to Slack

  • Successfully implemented Slack as AfriVate's official internal communication platform.
  • Established Slack as the primary channel for official workplace communication and collaboration.
  • Officially discontinued WhatsApp as a mode of official workplace communication.
  • Communicated the transition and requirements to employees.

Employee Slack Onboarding

  • Followed up individually with employees to ensure they successfully joined the AfriVate Slack workspace.
  • Assisted employees with setting up and configuring their Slack accounts.
  • Ensured employees understood the new communication structure and expectations.

B. Employee Portal Onboarding

  • Successfully onboarded all employees onto the AfriVate portal for work-related functions and processes.
  • Followed up where necessary to ensure employees were able to access and use the relevant portal functions.
  • Supported the broader transition towards a more centralized and structured system for managing AfriVate's work processes.

C. Team Leadership & Organizational Structure

Pseudo Team Lead Onboarding

  • Facilitated the onboarding of Joel as the new Pseudo Team Lead for the Development Team.
  • Facilitated the onboarding of Endurance as the new Pseudo Team Lead for the Media & Research Team.
  • Provided the necessary orientation and context to help both team leads understand their responsibilities, expectations, and position within the organization.

D. Recruitment & Workforce Development

Republished recruitment posts to attract qualified candidates and support the filling of current staffing needs for:

  • Content Creator
  • Front-End Developer
  • Back-End Developer
  • Graphic Designer

The recruitment exercise is aimed at strengthening the relevant teams and addressing existing workforce requirements.

  • Transitioning employees from an existing communication platform to a new official platform required repeated communication, reminders, and individual follow-ups.

  • The move from WhatsApp to Slack marks a significant step towards creating a more structured and professional internal communication environment.
  • Centralizing official communication on Slack should make departmental collaboration, announcements, direct communication, and work discussions easier to manage.
  • The successful onboarding of all employees onto the portal demonstrates improved adoption of AfriVate's internal work systems.
  • The introduction of pseudo team leads provides an additional layer of leadership and accountability within key teams.
  • The current recruitment drive is important for addressing staffing gaps and improving the capacity of the Development and Media & Research teams, alongside other operational functions.
  • The transition also highlighted the importance of clear communication, consistent follow-up, and enforcement when introducing mandatory organizational processes.

7. Overall Weekly Summary

The week of 10–14 August 2026 recorded substantial progress across AfriVate's HR and organizational operations. The successful transition to Slack, discontinuation of WhatsApp for official communication, onboarding of all employees onto the AfriVate portal, and individual support provided during the transition have strengthened the organization's internal communication structure.

The onboarding of Joel and Endurance as pseudo team leads also represents progress towards strengthening team leadership and accountability, while the republishing of four recruitment opportunities has initiated the process of addressing current staffing needs.

Overall, the week was focused on building stronger systems, improving organizational structure, reinforcing employee compliance, and strengthening AfriVate's workforce capacity. Several initiatives have been successfully completed, while recruitment, team-lead integration, and continued adoption of the new systems remain ongoing priorities.
