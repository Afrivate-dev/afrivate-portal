Dear Team,

As part of AfriVate’s continuous learning and professional development initiative, all assigned team members must complete the following learning assignment on or before **Friday, 31 July 2026**.

**Your learning journey:**  
🔎 Explore the AfriVate resources

## 1. Review AfriVate Resources

Open the **Resources** section of the AfriVate Portal and carefully review every document made available to you. Complete any required policy acknowledgements.

The resources cover AfriVate’s culture and expectations, design and development standards, recruitment and careers processes, internal workflows, policies, and other materials relevant to your role.

## 2. Complete the Alison Course

**Course:** Professionalism in the Office  
**Platform:** Alison  
**Course link:** https://alison.com/course/professionalism-in-the-office

If you do not already have an Alison account:

1. Visit https://alison.com.
2. Select **Sign Up**.
3. Register with your preferred email address, preferably the email used for AfriVate communication.
4. Verify your email if prompted.
5. Search for **Professionalism in the Office** or use the direct link above.
6. Complete every module.

After completing the course, download your **Alison Learner Record** as evidence of completion.

Tutorial: https://youtu.be/FYN3OmliNH4?si=2U4k3mgyhImqsL8D

## 3. Prepare Your Learning Report

Submit either:

• A document of 1 to 2 pages; or  
• A presentation of 4 to 5 slides.

Your report must address:

1. **Workplace Professionalism: Key Lessons:** Summarise lessons from the AfriVate resources and the *Professionalism in the Office* course. Focus on professional conduct, communication, accountability, time management, workplace etiquette, collaboration, integrity, and how these behaviours influence work quality.
2. **Applying Professionalism in Daily Work:** Explain how you will apply the course lessons in your current role at AfriVate. Give practical examples covering ClickUp communication, punctuality, deadlines, accountability, teamwork, handling feedback, resolving disagreements respectfully, protecting company information, and maintaining accurate Portal records.
3. **Personal Professional Development Plan:** Identify three professional behaviours or habits you will improve over the next 30 days. For each one, state the action you will take, how it will improve your work or team, and how you will measure progress.

## Submission

Email the following to **afrivatehr@gmail.com**:

• Your Alison Learner Record.

**Required submission subject:**
