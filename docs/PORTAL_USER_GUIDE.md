# AfriVate Team Space — Portal User Guide

**Product:** AfriVate Team Space  
**Live site:** [portal.afrivate.org](https://portal.afrivate.org)  
**Audience:** Every team member, lead, People & Culture, and administrator  
**Last updated:** August 2026

This guide describes the principal features of AfriVate Team Space and the approved procedures for their use.

---

## 1. How AfriVate uses this portal

| Tool | What it is for |
|------|----------------|
| **Portal (Team Space)** | The system of record for leave, policies, learning evidence, surveys, recognition, growth plans, memos, appraisals, discipline, PIPs, and employee profiles. |
| **Email (`hr@afrivate.org`)** | Official organisational broadcasts and correspondence. Responses should ordinarily be issued within one (1) working day. |
| **Alison** | Course delivery and certificates. Assignments and evidence of completion are recorded in the Portal. |
| **Google Drive** | Master copies of policies and contracts. Staff-facing copies are published under **Resources**. |
| **Slack** | Official internal messaging for clarification, coordination, escalation, and reachability during core hours. Official messages must be acknowledged within four (4) hours on official work days. Slack does **not** replace Portal records. |
| **WhatsApp** | Informal or emergency contact only. WhatsApp must **not** be used for leave requests, policy acknowledgements, appraisals, or formal People & Culture processes. |

**Operating principle:** Matters requiring a formal record, approval, or acknowledgement must be completed in the Portal. Official broadcasts are issued by email. Learning delivery is provided through Alison. Official internal messaging is conducted through Slack. WhatsApp is reserved for informal or emergency contact only.

## 2. Roles at a glance

| Role you see | Typical authority |
|--------------|-------------------------|
| **Team member** | Access core employee features: tasks, leave requests, learning, surveys, My info, recognition, and growth tools. |
| **Assistant lead** | All team-member capabilities, plus leave approval for managed personnel and visibility of team check-ins. |

Leads ordinarily have visibility of personnel linked through reports-to, team-lead, or department-head relationships.

## 3. Getting access

### Request access
1. Open **Request access** on the login screen (`/request-access`).
2. Enter your name, work email, password, department, and job title.
3. Submit the request. Access remains pending until People & Culture or an Administrator approves it under **Admin → Approvals**.

### Sign in
1. Go to `/login`.
2. Sign in with email and password, or use a magic link if offered.
3. If the account remains pending, a waiting state is displayed until approval is granted.

### Forgot or reset password
1. Use **Forgot password** (`/forgot-password`).
2. Open the email link and set a new password on `/reset-password`.
3. Sign in again.

### Account & security
- Open **Account** (`/account`) to change email or password.
- Read the privacy notice anytime at `/privacy`.

## 5. Home

**Path:** Home (`/`)

Home presents a personal overview and items requiring attention:
- Tasks due, leave waiting on you (if you are a lead), unread memos, today’s events
- Banners for open surveys, learning due, or policies that need acknowledgment
- Early onboarding progress if you joined recently

**Procedure:** Select a card or banner to open the relevant page. Review recent memos and mark them as read upon completion.

---

## 6. My work (Tasks)

**Path:** My work (`/tasks`)

Track personal and assigned work with status, priority, due dates, categories, hours, and blockers. When someone marks a task complete, the portal stores **who** completed it and **when**.

**Procedure:**
1. Filter by Mine / Owned / Assigned, status, priority, or category.
2. Switch views (board, list, week) if available.
3. Create a task: title, description, assignees, due date, estimate, hours, blockers.
4. Move status: To do → In progress → Done (or Blocked).
5. Open a task to log activity or mark **Complete**.

---

## 7. Inbox & Search

### Inbox (`/inbox`)
Shows notifications (shout-outs, assignments, mentions, and similar). Open an item to mark it read and jump to the related page. Use **Mark all as read** to clear outstanding notification indicators.

### Search (`/search`)
Enter a name or keyword. Results can include people, memos, tasks, documents, events, leave, shout-outs, surveys, learning, OKRs, and (for HR) recruitment and related HR records. Visibility respects your role.

---

## 8. Weekly update (Check-in)

**Path:** Weekly update (`/checkin`)

Each week, share what you finished, what is next, blockers, and hours worked. You can usually set visibility (department or wider).

**Procedure:**
1. Open **This week** / submit form.
2. Fill the fields and submit (you can update the same week later).
3. Use **History** for your past weeks.
4. Leads use **Team** to review their people’s updates. HR can also review org-wide under **Admin → Check-ins**.

## 9. Getting started (Onboarding)

**Path:** Getting started (`/onboarding`)

For new joiners: welcome videos and a checklist.

**Procedure:** Complete the assigned videos and checklist items in sequence. People & Culture manages videos and checklist items under **Admin → Onboarding**.

## 10. Notes

**Path:** Notes (`/notes`)

Shared pages for meeting notes, plans, and knowledge.

**Procedure:** Create or edit pages, set sharing to team or private as appropriate, and delete only where authorised and confirmed.

## 11. Memos (Announcements)

**Path:** Memos (`/announcements`)

Company and department updates with priority, categories, and optional attachments. Some digests mirror what HR also sends by email.

**Procedure:**
1. Browse and filter memos; open to read and mark as read.
2. **Team lead and above** can compose or edit memos (drafts may save on your device before publish).
3. Set audience and priority; attach files when needed.
4. HR/Admin can moderate and delete; HR can draft digests from the HR dashboard.

---

## 12. Resources (Document library)

**Path:** Resources (`/documents`)

Handbooks, policies, and templates. Some documents require you to **acknowledge** that you have read them.

**Procedure:**
1. Browse or search by category.
2. Open, preview, or download.
3. If a policy requires acknowledgment, complete that action (Home and People will remind you).
4. HR uploads and manages documents, categories, and flags (requires acknowledgment, HR-only, management-only).

## 13. Calendar (Events calendar)

**Path:** Calendar (`/events`)

Team events in month / week / day / agenda views. A Google Calendar view may appear if configured.

**Procedure:** Browse events and open details. **Team lead and above** can create and publish events (all staff or a department). Drafts may save locally before you publish.

## 14. People hub — day-to-day people tools

### Overview (`/people`)
Shortcuts into leave, shout-outs, learning, surveys, growth, My info, and directory. Leads and HR also see team or org snapshot cards and action banners.

### Time off (`/people/leave`)
This is the **official** leave channel (annual, sick, emergency, and related types).

**How to request leave:**
1. Click **Request leave**.
2. Choose dates, type, and reason.
3. Attach a supporting document if needed (for example a medical note).
4. Submit and track status under **My requests**.

**How leads / HR approve:**
1. Open **Team requests** or **All requests** (HR), or use **Admin → Leave**.
2. Approve or decline with a note.
3. Use the calendar tab to see time off at a glance.

### Shout-outs (`/people/shout-outs`)
Peer recognition tied to values or tags.

**Procedure:** Submit recognition entries, respond with likes or comments where appropriate, and share within permitted channels. People & Culture administers recognition tags.

### Learning (`/people/learning`)
Monthly Alison assignments and certificate proof.

**Procedure:**
1. Open the assigned Alison course.
2. When finished, submit course name, completion date, and certificate.
3. Track pending / approved / rejected.
4. HR publishes assignments and reviews submissions (also from the HR dashboard).

### Surveys (`/people/surveys`)
Pulse surveys, eNPS, and onboarding surveys. One response per person feeds HR metrics.

**Procedure:** Open an active survey, answer the questions, submit. HR launches surveys from templates, sends reminders, and reviews results under **Admin → HR dashboard**.

### Growth (`/people/growth`)
Performance and voice tools in one place:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **OKRs** | Quarterly objectives and key results |
| **1:1s** | Monthly manager check-ins (meet outside the portal; mark complete here) |
| **IDP** | Individual development plan — write, submit, respond to manager notes |
| **Feedback** | 360° forms when a cycle is open |
| **Milestones** | Day 30 / 60 / 90 onboarding checkpoints |
| **Awards** | Quarterly award visibility |
| **Speak up** | Confidential grievances |

Managers mark 1:1s complete for their reports. HR reviews IDPs, opens feedback cycles, resolves grievances, and runs awards from Admin.

### My info (`/people/my-info`)
Your personal details for the Employee Information Hub.

**What you can update:** Preferred and legal name, personal email, phone, work location, country, date of birth, pronouns, LinkedIn, bio, skills, emergency contact, and next-of-kin notes.

**What you cannot change yourself:** Employment status, engagement type, probation dates, payroll flags, and other HR-only fields. Those live under **Admin → Employees**.

**Procedure:** Complete the form, review the completeness indicator, and select **Save**. Where People & Culture requests a review, a reminder banner is displayed.

### Directory (`/people/directory`)
Search the team, open profiles, see availability, and contact colleagues. Edit your own profile card; HR/Admin can edit others when needed.

## 15. Admin — for People & Culture and Administrators

**Path:** Admin (`/admin`) — only **HR** and **Admin** roles see this in the sidebar.

### Approvals
Review pending access requests. **Approve** (set role, department, job title as prompted) or **Deny**. Invite a member by email when needed.

### Recruitment
Applicant tracking for open roles: criteria, Gmail sync from the HR mailbox, ranking, stages (applied through hired/rejected), CV preview, search, and rescoring. Connect Gmail via the OAuth flow when prompted.

### Users
View and edit people (department, job title, active flag, reports-to). **Only Administrators** can change roles. You cannot deactivate your own signed-in account.

### Departments & Teams (Admin only)
Create and maintain departments (optional head) and teams (lead, assistant lead, department link).

### Announcements
Moderate org-wide memos from Admin when needed.

### Leave
Org leave queue and calendar-style review — approve or decline pending requests.

### Onboarding
Manage welcome videos and checklist items for Getting started.

### Check-ins
Org-wide weekly check-in digest.

### HR dashboard
People operations overview covering headcount, engagement, eNPS, learning, 1:1 rate, leave, grievances, surveys, attrition, time-to-hire, policy acknowledgments, OKRs, recognition, onboarding CSAT, **active PIPs**, **pending discipline**, and **probation due**.

From here you can also:
- Export KPIs
- Assign Alison courses and review certificates
- Launch pulse / eNPS surveys and remind non-responders
- Manage awards, IDP review, policy acknowledgments, exit interviews, grievances
- Open 360° feedback cycles and manage templates
- Draft an HR digest memo
- Adjust portal labels (categories and tags)
- Open the Revival launch checklist if you are on the allowlist

### Employees (Employee Information Hub)
System of record for profiles, progressive discipline, PIPs, appraisals, probation, and offboarding.

#### Directory
Search active people by engagement type, employment status, and profile completeness. Open a **dossier** for full detail.

#### Person dossier
- Set engagement (employee / volunteer / contractor) and status (active, probation, leave, exiting, terminated, archived)
- Record start date, probation end, confirmation, payroll setup, contract or volunteer terms
- Keep HR-private notes (these stay off the default PDF)
- **Request employee to update My Info**
- Review discipline history and open a new case
- **Export PDF** (optionally include discipline) and **Archive** when appropriate

#### Discipline & PIP
Follow AfriVate’s progressive ladder:
1. Coaching / verbal
2. Written warning
3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
4. Restricted duties
5. Termination case

Record severity, trigger (for example missed deadlines, communication, absence, misconduct, underperformance), and how it was delivered (portal notice, meeting, email, letter).

- **Activate (HR)** or save as a **pending recommendation** for HR approval
- Approve pending items in the queue (termination remains carefully gated)
- For active PIPs: track goals and review cadence, mark on track, and close with passed / extended / escalated / terminated recommendation
- Creating a PIP step can auto-create a PIP with templated goals and duration

#### Appraisals
Formal appraisals use **60% output / deliverables** and **40% soft skills / behavioural**. Scores roll into an overall percentage and band (Exceptional, Good, Performance concern, Disciplinary, Termination consideration — as configured in the portal). Cadence is typically quarterly, or monthly while someone is on an active PIP. Save, finalize, and keep history on the person’s record. Pair with the branded **Performance Appraisal Form** under `docs/official/policies/` when you need a printable worksheet.

#### Probation
See upcoming probation end dates and **Confirm** employees into active status with confirmation metadata.

#### Lead scorecards
Track lead quality signals such as delivery consistency, KPI completion, communication discipline, and escalation quality.

#### Offboarding
Start a checklist with a reason. Default items cover completing or reassigning Portal tasks, final check-in, knowledge transfer, revoking access (email, Slack, Portal), collecting assets, exit interview notes, and volunteer Code of Conduct bridge notices when relevant. Tick items as done.

#### Audit
Chronological log of HR actions on profiles, discipline, PIPs, appraisals, and offboarding.

## 18. Recommended weekly practice

1. Begin at **Home** and address outstanding banners (surveys, learning, policy acknowledgements).
2. Record leave and formal People & Culture matters in the **Portal** — not in WhatsApp or Slack.
3. Mark tasks **Complete** in My work so completion time and actor are recorded.
4. Submit the **Weekly update** before the reporting period closes.
5. Maintain complete **My info** records to minimise administrative follow-up.
6. Use **Slack** for official internal messaging. Use the Portal whenever a formal record, approval, or acknowledgement is required.
