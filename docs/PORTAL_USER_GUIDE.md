## What this portal is (in one minute)

AfriVate Team Space is the **official work website** for the team. Think of it as the one place where important work is written down, approved, and kept.

If something needs a **record** (leave, a weekly update, a learning certificate, a policy you have read, an appraisal, a PIP), it belongs here.

If something is just a **chat** (a quick question, a reminder, “are you around?”), use **Slack**.

If something is an **emergency** or informal, WhatsApp is fine. WhatsApp is **not** the place for leave, appraisals, or official People & Culture processes.

## 1. The golden rule: which tool to use

| Tool | Use it for | Do not use it for |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| **Portal (Team Space)** | Anything that must be recorded: leave, weekly updates, tasks, learning proof, surveys, shout-outs, growth plans, memos, appraisals, discipline, PIPs, your personal details, and signing that you have read a policy. | Casual chat. |
| **Slack** | Official day-to-day messaging: questions, coordination, escalation, and being reachable in core hours. Reply to official Slack messages within **four (4) hours** on official work days. | Leave requests, policy acknowledgements, appraisals, or any process that needs a Portal record. A Slack message does **not** count as a Portal submission. |
| **Email (`hr@afrivate.org`)** | Official company broadcasts and formal correspondence. Replies should usually come within one (1) working day. | Day-to-day team chat. |
| **Alison** | Taking the assigned online course. | Proving you finished it — that proof goes in the Portal. |
| **Google Drive** | Master copies of policies and contracts. | The everyday staff copy — those live under **Resources** in the Portal. |
| **WhatsApp** | Informal or emergency contact only. | Leave, policy acknowledgements, appraisals, or any formal People & Culture process. |
| **AVA** | Asking “how do I…?” and being taken to the right page. | Completing work for you. AVA never submits, approves, or deletes anything. You always click the button yourself. |

**Remember:** If it needs a stamp of “this happened, and here is the proof,” use the Portal.

## 2. Who can do what

Everyone sees a **role** on their profile. That role decides what extra buttons you get.

| Role you see | What you can do |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Team member** | Everyday work: tasks, leave requests, weekly updates, learning, surveys, shout-outs, My info, growth tools, notes, memos (read), resources, calendar. |
| **Assistant lead** | Everything a team member can do, **plus** approve leave for the people you manage, and see their weekly updates. |
| **Team lead** | Everything an assistant lead can do, **plus** publish memos and add calendar events. |
| **People & culture (HR)** | Everything above, **plus** the **Admin** area: approvals, leave for the whole organisation, the HR dashboard, Recruitment, and the Employee hub. |
| **Administrator** | Everything HR can do, **plus** change someone’s role, and manage departments and teams. |

Leads usually see the people who report to them, the people on their team, or the people in a department they head.

Only **Administrators** can change a person’s role.

## 3. Getting into the portal

**Website:** [portal.afrivate.org](https://portal.afrivate.org)

### 3.1 Request access (first time)

You cannot just walk in. Someone from People & Culture (or an Administrator) must approve you.

1. On the login screen, click **Request access**.
2. Enter your full name, work email, and a password.
3. Choose your department and type your job title.
4. You may add a short message if you like.
5. Click submit.

**Password rules:** at least 8 characters, with one uppercase letter, and one number or symbol.

After you submit, you wait. You may be able to sign in, but you will see a “waiting for approval” screen until HR or Admin clicks **Approve** under **Admin → Approvals**.

### 3.2 Sign in

1. Go to the portal and open **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email and password, then click sign in.
3. Or use a **magic link**: enter your email, ask for a link, open the email, and click it. No password needed that time.
4. If your account is not approved yet, you will see a waiting screen.

### 3.3 Forgot your password

1. On the login screen, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email.
3. Open the email you receive and click the link.
4. Set a new password (same password rules as above).
5. Sign in again.

### 3.4 Change email or password later

1. Click your name or photo in the top-right corner.
2. Open **Account & security**.
3. Change your email (you will confirm via email) or set a new password.
4. You can also send yourself a verification email if the portal asks you to confirm it is still you.

### 3.5 Privacy

You can read the privacy notice at any time from **Account** or by opening `/privacy`.

### 3.6 Sign out

Click your name or photo in the top-right → **Sign out**. Confirm when asked.

### 4.2 On a phone

The bottom bar shows **Home**, **My work**, **Inbox**, **People**, and **More**.

Tap **More** (or the menu icon at the top) to open the full list: Weekly update, Getting started, Notes, Memos, Resources, Calendar, and Admin (if you have it).

### 4.3 The top bar (every page)

Across the top of every page you will find:

- **Dark / light mode** — the sun or moon icon. Tap it to switch between a light screen and a dark screen.
- **Search** — jumps to the Search page.
- **Availability** — tell colleagues whether you are Available, Away, Busy, or Focusing. This shows on the Directory.
- **Live / Presence offline** — “Live” means the portal can show who is online right now. If it says presence is offline, availability may not update for others until it reconnects.
- **Bell** — Inbox. A red dot means you have unread items.
- **Your photo / name** — opens **My profile**, **Account & security**, and **Sign out**.

### 4.4 Install the portal like an app

On some phones and computers (Chrome, Edge, Android), a banner may appear: **Install AfriVate Portal**. If you tap **Install**, the portal sits on your home screen like a normal app. You can dismiss the banner if you prefer the browser.

### 4.5 AVA (the purple helper)

A purple **AVA** button sits in the bottom-right on a computer, and just above the bottom menu on a phone. See [section 23](#23-ava--the-in-portal-helper).

## 5. Home

**Where:** Home (the first page after you sign in)

Home is your “good morning” page. It greets you by name and shows what needs attention.

**What you will see**

- Shortcut buttons: **New task**, **Request leave**, **Submit check-in**.
- Numbers for: tasks due today, leave waiting (yours, or yours to approve if you are a lead), unread memos, and events coming in the next 7 days.
- Coloured reminder banners if you have an open survey, a learning certificate due, or a policy you have not yet acknowledged.
- If you joined in the last 30 days: a **Getting started** progress bar for onboarding videos.
- Today’s events.
- The latest memos (open one to read it and mark it as read).

**What to do**

1. Start here each work day.
2. Tap any banner or card — it takes you to the right page.
3. Clear banners by actually doing the thing (submit the survey, upload the certificate, acknowledge the policy).

## 6. My work (tasks)

**Where:** My work

This is the shared to-do list. You can create work, assign it to people, track status, and mark it complete. When someone marks a task complete, the portal stores **who** did it and **when**.

**Views**

- **Board** — columns like a kanban board (Up next → In progress → Complete / Stuck).
- **List** — grouped by due date (overdue, today, this week, later).
- **Week** — laid out across the current week.

**Filters**

You can filter by:

- **Mine / Owned / Assigned** — work you own, work assigned to you, or both.
- Status (up next, in progress, complete, stuck).
- Priority (urgent, normal, later).
- Category (the work type, set up by the organisation).
- Search by title.

**How to create a task**

1. Click **New task** (from Home or My work).
2. Fill in a title (required). Add a description if it helps.
3. Choose assignees (who should do it), a due date, priority, category, estimated hours, hours already spent, and any blockers.
4. Save.

**How to update a task**

1. Open the task (click the card).
2. Change status, add comments / activity, update hours or blockers.
3. Move it: **Up next → In progress → Complete**. If you are stuck, mark it **Stuck**.
4. When the work is finished, mark **Complete**. Do not just tell someone on Slack — the Portal timestamp is the official record.

**Tips**

- Use **Stuck** when something is blocking you, and write what the blocker is.
- Completing a task in the Portal is how AfriVate knows the work was actually done.

## 7. Inbox

**Where:** Inbox (or the bell in the top bar)

Inbox is not email. It is a list of **portal alerts** for you.

Typical alerts include:

- Someone gave you a **shout-out**, or commented on one.
- A **task** was assigned to you, or someone mentioned you on a task or note.
- Your **leave** was approved, declined, or someone left a comment on it.
- A **survey** reminder.
- A **memo** was published.
- Your **access** was approved or denied (or, for HR, someone requested access).
- Your **department** changed.

**What to do**

1. Open Inbox.
2. Tap an item — it is marked as read and you jump to the related page.
3. Use **Mark all as read** if you have cleared them in your head and want the red dot gone.

## 8. Search

**Where:** Search (or the search icon in the top bar)

Type a person’s name, a memo title, a task, a document name, or any keyword.

Results can include: people, memos, tasks, notes, documents, events, leave, shout-outs, surveys, learning, OKRs, and (for HR) recruitment, grievances, awards, and related records.

You only see what your role is allowed to see. Staff will not see private HR files through Search.

## 9. Weekly update

**Where:** Weekly update

Every week you tell the team four simple things:

1. What you **finished**.
2. What you will do **next**.
3. Anything **blocking** you.
4. How many **hours** you worked.

You can choose who sees it: your **department**, or **everyone**.

**How to submit**

1. Open **Weekly update**. Stay on **This week**.
2. Fill in the four fields.
3. Choose visibility.
4. Submit. You can come back and edit the same week later.
5. Open **History** to see your past weeks.

**If you are a lead**

Open the **Team** tab. You will see who on your team has submitted and who has not. You can filter by department.

**If you are HR**

You can also review the whole organisation under **Admin → Check-ins**.

This is the official weekly record. A Slack “I was busy this week” message is not a substitute.

## 11. Notes

**Where:** Notes

Notes are shared pages — meeting notes, plans, how-we-do-things write-ups. They can nest under other pages, like folders.

**How to use them**

1. Create a page and write in the editor.
2. Choose who can see it:
   - **Private** — only you (and admins as needed).
   - **Workspace** — everyone in the portal.
   - **Departments** or **Teams** — only those groups.
   - **People** — named colleagues.
3. You can share a link to a page when link sharing is turned on.
4. Delete only when you are sure — it is removed for everyone who had access.

If someone is looking at the same note at the same time, you may see a live presence indicator.

## 12. Memos

**Where:** Memos

Memos are official written updates from the company or a department. Some HR digests here are the same content HR also sends by email.

Each memo has a title, body (or an attached document that *is* the body), a category, a priority (**Info**, **Important**, or **Urgent**), an audience (everyone or a department), and optional pictures or files.

**How to read memos**

1. Open **Memos**.
2. Filter or search if the list is long.
3. Open a memo, read it, and mark it as read.
4. Home will keep reminding you about unread ones.

**Who can publish**

- **Team lead and above** can write and publish memos.
- Drafts can save on your own device until you are ready to publish.
- Set the audience (everyone or a department) and priority.
- Attach images or files when needed.
- **HR / Admin** can edit or delete memos from Admin as well.
- HR can draft an HR digest from the HR dashboard.

If your account is **suspended**, Memos is one of the two areas you can still read (the other is Resources).

## 13. Resources (policies and files)

**Where:** Resources

This is the filing cabinet: handbooks, policies, templates, and similar files.

Some files are open to everyone. Some are **HR only**. Some are **management only**. Some require you to click **I have read this** (acknowledge).

**How to use Resources as staff**

1. Browse by category, or search by name.
2. Open a file to preview it, or download it.
3. If the file needs acknowledgement, complete that action. Home and People will keep a yellow banner until you do.

**How HR manages Resources**

HR can upload files (from the computer or Google Drive), set the category, and tick:

- Requires acknowledgement
- HR only
- Management only

HR can also rename categories and see who has (and has not) acknowledged a policy, from the HR dashboard.

**This is the staff-facing copy.** The master legal copy may live in Google Drive. If they differ, follow what People & Culture tells you.

If your account is **suspended**, you can still read Resources.

## 16. Time off (leave)

**Where:** People → Time off

This is the **only official way** to request leave. A WhatsApp message, a Slack ping, a phone call, or a verbal “I’m off tomorrow” is **not** an approved request (except a true emergency, which you still record here as soon as you can).

### Leave types and typical allowances (as shown in the portal)

| Type | Everyday meaning | Typical allowance shown |
|------|------------------|-------------------------|
| **Annual** | Planned holiday / vacation | 20 days |
| **Sick** | You are unwell | 10 days |
| **Emergency** | Sudden, unavoidable absence | 7 days |

Except accepted emergencies, give at least **three (3) official work days’ notice**. Finish or hand over your work, and tell colleagues on Slack, **before** you go.

### How to request leave

1. Open **People → Time off** (or Home → **Request leave**, or ask AVA → **Go to Time off**).
2. Click **Request leave**.
3. Choose start date, end date, type (annual / sick / emergency), and a reason.
4. Attach a supporting file if needed (for example a medical note). You can upload a file or pick one from Google Drive.
5. Confirm, then submit.
6. Watch status under **My requests**: pending → approved or declined.
7. You and your manager can leave comments on the request.

### How leads approve leave

1. Open **People → Time off**.
2. Use **Team requests** (or **All requests** if you are HR).
3. Open the request. Read the dates, reason, and any attachment.
4. **Approve** or **Decline**, and add a short note.
5. Use the **Calendar** tab to see who is off at a glance.

HR can also approve from **Admin → Leave**.

Until the Portal says **approved**, you are not on approved leave.

## 17. Shout-outs

## 18. Learning (Alison courses)

**Where:** People → Learning

AfriVate assigns courses on **Alison** (an external learning website). You take the course there. You **prove you finished** it here.

**How to complete an assignment**

1. Open **People → Learning**.
2. Open the assigned Alison course (it will have a link).
3. Finish the course on Alison and download or photograph your certificate.
4. Back in the Portal, enter the course name, the completion date, and upload the certificate (or pick it from Google Drive).
5. Submit. Status will be **pending** until HR reviews it.
6. If HR **rejects** it, fix what they asked for and resubmit.

Home and People will show a banner until your proof is in.

**HR** publishes assignments and reviews certificates from this page and from **Admin → HR dashboard**.

## 19. Surveys

**Where:** People → Surveys

From time to time HR opens a short survey:

- **Pulse** — how are you finding work right now?
- **eNPS** — would you recommend AfriVate as a place to work?
- **Onboarding** — how was your first period here?

One response per person. Your individual answers go to HR. Leads may see **combined** team scores, not your personal answers.

**How to complete a survey**

1. Open **People → Surveys**, or tap the Home / People banner.
2. Open the active survey.
3. Answer the questions and submit.

HR launches surveys, sends reminders, and reads results under **Admin → HR dashboard**.

## 20. Growth (OKRs, 1:1s, IDP, and more)

**Where:** People → Growth

This is your performance and voice area. It has several tabs.

### OKRs

OKRs means **Objectives and Key Results** — “what I am aiming at this quarter” and “how we will know I got there.”

Add an objective and one or more key results for the current quarter. Update them as you progress.

### 1:1s

A 1:1 is a regular conversation with your manager (usually monthly). You meet **outside** the portal (call, Slack huddle, in person). Then the manager marks the 1:1 **complete** here so HR can see that the conversation happened.

### IDP

IDP means **Individual Development Plan** — your written plan for growing skills.

Write the plan, submit it, and respond if your manager leaves notes. HR can review IDPs from Admin.

### Feedback (360°)

When HR opens a feedback cycle, you may be asked to rate:

- Yourself (**self-assessment**)
- Your manager (**upward**)
- A peer
- Someone who reports to you (**manager review**)

Complete the form while the cycle is open. HR manages templates and cycles from the HR dashboard.

### Milestones

Day **30 / 60 / 90** checkpoints for new joiners. Tick them when you have had that conversation or review.

### Awards

When quarterly awards are announced, you can see winners here.

### Speak up (grievances)

A confidential way to raise a workplace concern.

1. Open **Speak up**.
2. Choose a category and write what happened.
3. Submit. HR receives it. You can see the status of your own submissions.

This is for real concerns, not for leave requests or IT niggles.

## 23. AVA — the in-portal helper

**Where:** Purple **AVA** button (bottom-right on a computer; above the phone menu)

AVA means **AfriVate Virtual Assistant**. She is a help desk inside the portal — not a manager, and not a robot that does your work.

**She can**

- Answer “how do I request leave?” and similar how-to questions.
- Explain the four-hour Slack rule, which tool to use, and other published policy in short form.
- Show a **Go to…** button that opens the right page (Time off, Learning, Weekly update, and so on).
- Use a snapshot of *your* open tasks, leave, and surveys so the answer fits you.

**She cannot**

- Submit, approve, reject, edit, or delete anything for anyone.
- Fill in a leave form or weekly update and save it for you.
- Replace a People & Culture decision.
- See records you are not allowed to see.
- Give salary, legal, or confidential HR advice. For those, email `hr@afrivate.org`.

**How to use AVA**

1. Click **AVA**.
2. Type a question, or tap a suggested prompt (for example “How do I request leave?”).
3. Read the steps.
4. If a **Go to…** button appears, click it.
5. Complete the action yourself on that page.
6. Close AVA with the close button or the Escape key.

If AVA is down, use this User Guide under **Resources**, or email `hr@afrivate.org`.

AVA may gently nudge you if you have been idle on a page — you can ignore or close that.

## 24. PIP and suspension (duty status)

People & Culture (and Administrators) can set a person’s **duty status**. This is separate from the everyday “Available / Away” control.

| Status | What it means for the person | Who can see the badge |
|--------|------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Normal duty** | Ordinary access. No special badge. | — |
| **On PIP** | You are on a Performance Improvement Plan. You **keep full portal access**. A banner reminds you. Your lead, HR, and admin can see the badge. | You, team leads, HR, admin |
| **Suspended** | You can still **sign in**. You can read **Memos** and **Resources**, and manage **Account & security**. You **cannot** use the rest of the portal (tasks, leave, check-ins, People tools, and so on) until HR lifts it. A red banner explains this. | You, team leads, HR, admin |

HR sets this on the person’s Employee hub dossier or under **Admin → Users**. Placing someone on PIP here does not automatically write the full formal PIP paperwork — that still happens under **Discipline & PIP** when needed.

If you are suspended and you try to open another page, the portal sends you back to Memos.

## 25. Admin — for People & Culture and Administrators

**Where:** Admin (only **HR** and **Admin** roles see this in the menu)

Admin is the back office. Staff do not see it.

The tabs along the top are:

Approvals · Recruitment · Users · Departments · Teams · Announcements · Leave · Onboarding · Check-ins · HR dashboard · Employees

**Departments** and **Teams** are **Administrator only**.

### 25.1 Approvals

People who requested access wait here.

1. Open the request. Check name, email, department, job title.
2. Set the right **role**, department, and job title if needed.
3. Click **Approve** (they can use the portal) or **Deny** (they stay out and can request again later).
4. You can also **invite** someone by email if they have not requested yet.

### 25.2 Users

The list of portal accounts.

You can:

- Search people.
- Edit department, job title, who they report to, and whether the account is **active**.
- Set **duty status** (normal / on PIP / suspended) — you will be asked to confirm.
- **Administrators only** can change **role**.
- You cannot deactivate **your own** signed-in account.

Deactivating someone means they can no longer sign in. That is different from **suspension**, where they can still sign in with a locked-down view.

### 25.3 Departments (Administrator only)

Create, rename, or delete departments. Optionally assign a **department head**. Assigning a person to a department can update their reporting line to that head.

### 25.4 Teams (Administrator only)

Create teams with a **lead**, optional **assistant lead**, and a link to a department. Add or remove members. A person’s department may align with the team’s department when you add them.

### 25.5 Announcements

Moderate organisation-wide memos from Admin: create, edit, delete, set audience and priority. Same content as the Memos page, from the HR/Admin side.

### 25.6 Leave

The organisation-wide leave queue and calendar. Approve or decline pending requests, add a note, and (when authorised) delete a request. Same decisions as People → Time off, with a full-org view.

### 25.7 Onboarding

Add, edit, or remove welcome **videos** (YouTube links, section, duration) and **checklist** items that new joiners see under Getting started.

### 25.8 Check-ins

A digest of everyone’s weekly updates. Filter by week or department. This is how HR sees who submitted and what they wrote.

### 25.9 HR dashboard

The people-operations overview. Numbers typically include:

Headcount · engagement · eNPS · learning completion · 1:1 rate · leave · grievances · surveys · attrition · time-to-hire · policy acknowledgements · OKRs · recognition · onboarding CSAT · **active PIPs** · **pending discipline** · **probation due** · **people on suspension**

From this dashboard you can also:

- Export KPIs
- Assign Alison courses and review certificates
- Launch pulse / eNPS surveys, manage templates, and remind people who have not responded
- Manage awards, IDP review, policy acknowledgements, exit interviews, and grievances
- Open 360° feedback cycles, assign reviewers, and manage templates
- Draft an HR digest memo
- Adjust **portal labels** (categories and tags used around the site)
- Open the Revival launch checklist if you are on the allowlist

## 26. Employee hub (HR records)

**Where:** Admin → Employees

This is the **system of record** for each person’s employment file: profile, duty status, discipline, PIPs, appraisals, probation, lead scorecards, offboarding, and an audit log.

Tabs: Directory · Discipline & PIP · Appraisals · Probation · Lead scorecards · Offboarding · Audit

### 26.1 Directory and person dossier

Search active people by engagement type, employment status, and how complete their My info is. Open a **dossier** (the full file) for one person.

On a dossier you can:

- Set **duty status**: Normal duty, On PIP, or Suspended (with a confirmation).
- Set **engagement**: employee / volunteer / contractor.
- Set **status**: active, probation, on leave, exiting, terminated, archived.
- Record start date, probation end, confirmation date, payroll setup, contract or volunteer terms.
- Keep **HR-private notes** (these stay off the default PDF).
- Click **Request employee to update My Info** — they see a banner on My info.
- Review discipline history and open a new case.
- **Export PDF** (optionally include discipline).
- **Archive** when appropriate.

### 26.2 Discipline & PIP

AfriVate uses a stepped process:

1. Coaching / verbal
2. Written warning
3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
4. Restricted duties
5. Termination case

When you record a case, capture:

- **Severity** (low, medium, high, critical)
- **Trigger** (for example missed deadlines, communication, absence, misconduct, underperformance)
- **How it was delivered** (portal notice, meeting, formal email, written letter)

You can **Activate (HR)** immediately, or save a **pending recommendation** for HR to approve. Termination stays carefully gated.

For an **active PIP**:

- Track goals and review cadence
- Mark whether the person is on track
- Close with: passed / extended / escalated / terminated recommendation

Creating a PIP step can auto-create a PIP with templated goals and duration. Setting duty status to “On PIP” on the dossier is the **visible badge**; the Discipline tab is the **formal paperwork**. Close both when the plan ends.

### 26.3 Appraisals

Formal appraisals score two parts:

- **60%** output / deliverables (did they deliver the work?)
- **40%** soft skills / behaviour (how they work with people)

Those two scores become one overall percentage and a band:

| Overall score | Band |
|---------------|------|
| 70% or more | Exceptional |
| 60–69% | Good |
| 50–59% | Performance concern — coaching |
| 40–49% | Disciplinary / corrective |
| Below 40% | Termination consideration |

Usual cadence: **quarterly**, or **monthly** while someone is on an active PIP. Save drafts, finalise, and keep history on the person’s record. Use the branded Performance Appraisal Form as a printable worksheet if you need paper.

### 26.4 Probation

See who is approaching a probation end date. **Confirm** them into active status and store the confirmation details.

### 26.5 Lead scorecards

Track quality signals for leads — for example delivery consistency, KPI completion, communication discipline, and escalation quality.

### 26.6 Offboarding

When someone is leaving:

1. Start a checklist and pick a reason.
2. Work through the default items: finish or reassign Portal tasks, final weekly update, knowledge transfer, revoke access (email, Slack, Portal), collect assets, exit interview notes, and volunteer Code of Conduct notices when relevant.
3. Tick items as done.

### 26.7 Audit

A chronological log of HR actions on profiles, discipline, PIPs, appraisals, and offboarding. Use it when you need to see *who changed what, and when*.

## 27. Recruitment

**Where:** Admin → Recruitment

This is the applicant tracker for open roles. It is for People & Culture (and Admin), not for general staff.

**What you can do**

- Define scoring **criteria** for a role.
- Connect **Gmail** (the HR mailbox) so applications can sync in.
- Search candidates, preview CVs, and read the application email.
- Rank / rescore candidates against the criteria.
- Move people through stages: **Applied → Screen → Interview → Offer → Hired** or **Rejected**.

When the portal asks you to connect Gmail, complete the Google sign-in popup. Applications are a people-data record — treat them as confidential.

## 30. A good weekly habit

1. Open **Home**. Clear the banners (survey, learning, policy).
2. Put leave and any formal People & Culture matter in the **Portal** — not WhatsApp or Slack.
3. Keep **My work** honest. Mark tasks **Complete** so the time and the person are recorded.
4. Submit the **Weekly update** before the week closes.
5. Keep **My info** complete so HR does not have to chase you.
6. Use **Slack** for chat. Use the Portal whenever something must be on the record.
7. If you are lost, ask **AVA**, then do the click yourself on the page she opens.

## 31. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it usually means | What to do |
|--------------|----------------------|------------|
| Waiting / pending approval screen | Your access request is not approved yet. | Wait, or email `hr@afrivate.org`. |
| Red banner: account on **suspension** | HR has paused most portal actions. | You can still read Memos and Resources. Contact HR if you think this is a mistake. |
| Amber banner: you are on a **PIP** | You are on a Performance Improvement Plan. | Keep using the portal as usual. Talk to your lead and HR. |
| “We couldn’t load your data” | A temporary connection problem. | Click **Retry**. If it lasts, refresh the page or try again later. |
| “Some profile details could not be loaded” | Part of your profile failed to load. | Click **Refresh**. You can still use the portal. |
| “Your browser storage is full” | The browser ran out of local space (often old notes or drafts). | Dismiss the banner; clear old notes or drafts if you can. |
| AVA does not answer | The helper is unavailable. | Use this guide in **Resources**, or email `hr@afrivate.org`. |
| Magic / reset link expired | The email link is old. | Request a new link from the login screen. |
| Presence offline | Live “who is online” is down. | You can still work. Availability may not show for others until it returns. |
