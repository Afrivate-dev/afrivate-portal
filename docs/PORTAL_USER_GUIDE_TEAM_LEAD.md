# AfriVate Team Space — Team Lead Portal Guide

**Product:** AfriVate Team Space  
**Live site:** [portal.afrivate.org](https://portal.afrivate.org)  
**Audience:** Team leads and assistant leads  
**Last updated:** 14 August 2026  
**Document code:** AFRI-PUG-03  
**Branded PDF:** [docs/official/policies/Afrivate-Portal-User-Guide-Team-Lead.pdf](official/policies/Afrivate-Portal-User-Guide-Team-Lead.pdf)

This guide is **only for team leads and assistant leads**. It covers what you can see and do in the portal — your own work **and** the extra tools for the people you manage.

It does **not** cover Admin, recruitment, appraisals, discipline paperwork, or role changes. You will not see those. People & Culture use the full [AFRI-PUG-01](PORTAL_USER_GUIDE.md). Ordinary staff use [AFRI-PUG-02](PORTAL_USER_GUIDE_STAFF.md).

---

## 1. Who this is for

| Role on your profile | What this means in the portal |
|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Assistant lead** | You do everything a team member does, **plus** approve leave for your people, see their weekly updates, see a team snapshot, mark 1:1s, review their IDPs, see team OKRs, see PIP/suspension badges, and open management-only files. You **cannot** publish memos or add calendar events. |
| **Team lead** | Everything an assistant lead can do, **plus** publish memos and add calendar events. |

You still **do not** see **Admin** in the menu. That is correct. You cannot approve new joiners, change roles, open recruitment, write appraisals, or place someone on a PIP. Send those to People & Culture.

If nobody “belongs” to you yet (no reports-to, no team, no department head role), team tabs will look empty. Ask HR to set reporting lines in the Directory / Users list.

## 2. The golden rule

If it needs a record, it belongs in the **Portal**. Slack is for chat (acknowledge official Slack messages within **four (4) hours** on official work days). WhatsApp is informal or emergency only.

A Slack “please approve my leave” is **not** an approval. Click **Approve** in the Portal.

| Tool | You use it for |
|------|----------------|
| **Portal** | Your own records **and** leave approvals, team check-ins, 1:1 completion, IDP review, memos (team lead), calendar events (team lead). |
| **Slack** | Coordination, handover before leave, chasing a late weekly update *after* you have checked the Portal. |
| **Email (`hr@afrivate.org`)** | Escalations to People & Culture. |
| **Alison** | Your own courses. Proof goes in the Portal. |
| **AVA** | How-to help. She never approves or submits for you. |

## 5. Home (your extra numbers)

Same banners as staff (surveys, learning, policies), **plus**:

- A count of **leave waiting on you** (your people’s pending requests, and your own).
- Shortcuts: New task, Request leave, Submit check-in.

Start here. Clear your own banners. Then open Time off if the leave number is not zero.

## 6. Your own work (you still do all of this)

You are not exempt from staff habits.

- **My work** — create tasks, assign people, mark **Complete** (the portal stores who and when). Views: Board, List, Week.
- **Inbox** — your alerts (leave comments, shout-outs, assignments, memos).
- **Search** — people, memos, tasks, documents you may open (including management-only), events, leave in your scope, and similar. Not HR-only files or recruitment.
- **Weekly update** — submit **your** four fields (finished, next, blockers, hours). Then open the **Team** tab (section 8).
- **Getting started** — if you are new; you cannot manage the videos.
- **Notes** — create and share pages.
- **Shout-outs, Learning, Surveys, My info** — same as staff. Keep your own My info complete.
- **Directory** — edit **your** card. You cannot edit other people’s profiles (HR/Admin can). You **can** see PIP / suspension badges on others.

Details for those staff workflows match everyday use; the rest of this guide is the **lead** work.

## 7. Approve leave (the most important extra)

**Where:** People → Time off

Staff only see **My requests**. You also see **Team requests** and **Calendar**.

Leave is official **only** in the Portal. Do not “approve” on WhatsApp or Slack and skip the button here.

**How to approve or decline**

1. Open People → **Time off** → **Team requests**.
2. Open the request. Check dates, type (annual / sick / emergency), reason, and any attachment (for example a medical note).
3. Confirm **Approve** or **Decline**, and add a short note. The person is notified in their Inbox.
4. Use **Calendar** to see who is off that month.

You see people linked to you through reports-to, your team, or a department you head. You do not see the whole organisation (HR does, under Admin).

**What to expect from your people**

- Except emergencies, at least **three (3) official work days’ notice**.
- Work handed over; they should also tell the team on Slack.
- Until you click Approve, they are **not** on approved leave.

You cannot delete a leave request. People & Culture can.

Typical allowances shown in the portal: annual 20 days, sick 10, emergency 7.

## 8. Team weekly updates

**Where:** Weekly update → **Team**

See who on your team has submitted this week and who has not. Open a person’s update to read finished / next / blockers / hours. You can filter by department.

Chase missing updates on Slack if you like — but the **record** is this page. HR can also see org-wide check-ins; you cannot.

## 9. People overview — team snapshot and duty status

**Where:** People (Overview)

**My team snapshot** shows combined numbers for people you manage (headcount, engagement, eNPS, and similar). You do **not** see anyone’s individual survey answers. Those stay with HR.

If no numbers appear, reporting lines may not be set. Ask HR.

**PIP & suspension** (visible to leads, HR, and admin): a list of people currently on a Performance Improvement Plan or suspension.

| Status | What it means | What you can do |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| **On PIP** | They keep full portal access. A banner shows for them. | Support them. You cannot create or close the formal PIP file. That is HR, in Admin. |
| **Suspended** | They can sign in and read **Memos** and **Resources** only. | Do not expect tasks, leave, or check-ins from them until HR lifts it. |

You will also see these badges on Directory profiles. You cannot change duty status. Only People & Culture / Admin can (on the person’s HR file or Users list).

## 10. Growth as a manager

**Where:** People → Growth

You still have your **own** OKRs, IDP, feedback, milestones, awards, and Speak up. On top of that:

### OKRs

Your own objectives for the quarter, with key-result sliders. Below that, **Team OKRs (read-only)** — you can see your people’s aims and progress. You cannot edit theirs.

### 1:1s

Meet **outside** the portal (Google Meet, Slack huddle, in person). Then come here and **Mark done** for each person this month. That is how HR knows the conversation happened.

You will also see whether **your** manager has marked *your* 1:1 complete. You cannot mark your own.

### IDP

Write and submit **your** plan. If people who report to you have submitted theirs, an **IDP reviews** panel appears. Read the plan, write manager feedback, click **Mark reviewed**. They will see your note.

### Feedback

When HR opens a 360° cycle, complete the forms assigned to you (including as a manager). You cannot open or close a cycle. HR does that.

### Speak up

Your own confidential concerns go to HR. You do not receive other people’s Speak up submissions.

## 11. Publish memos (team lead only)

**Where:** Memos

**Assistant leads: skip this section.** You can read memos; you cannot publish.

**Team leads**

1. Open **Memos**. Compose a memo (title, body or attached document, category, priority: Info / Important / Urgent).
2. Choose audience: everyone, or a department.
3. Attach images or files if needed. Drafts can save on your device until you publish.
4. Publish. People will see it on Home and in Inbox.

You can edit memos you posted. You cannot delete other people’s memos — HR/Admin can. If you need an organisation-wide HR digest, ask People & Culture; they can draft one from the HR dashboard.

---

## 12. Add calendar events (team lead only)

**Where:** Calendar

**Assistant leads: skip this section.** You can browse events; you cannot add them.

**Team leads:** create an event (title, date, start/end time, location, audience: everyone or a department). Drafts can save locally before you publish. Staff will see it on Home (if it is soon) and on Calendar.

## 13. Resources (including management-only)

Browse, preview, download, and **acknowledge** policies that require it — same as staff.

**Extra:** files marked **management only** are visible to you. Staff cannot see them. Files marked **HR only** are still hidden from you.

You cannot upload, recategorise, or delete library files. People & Culture manage the library.

## 14. AVA

Same helper as staff. Ask “How do I approve leave?” and use **Go to Time off**. She never clicks Approve for you.

She can use a snapshot of your open tasks, leave, and surveys. She will not show you HR records you are not allowed to see.

## 17. A good weekly habit for leads

1. **Home** — your banners first, then any leave waiting on you.
2. **Time off → Team requests** — do not leave people pending.
3. **Weekly update** — submit yours, then check **Team** for gaps.
4. **Growth → 1:1s** — if you met someone this month, mark it done.
5. Keep **your** My info, learning, and surveys current — you model the behaviour.
6. Records in the Portal. Chat on Slack. Escalate to `hr@afrivate.org` when it is an HR decision.

## 18. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|--------------|------------|
| Team requests / Team tab empty | Ask HR to set reports-to, team, or department head. |
| Cannot publish a memo or add an event | You may be an **assistant lead**. Ask a team lead or HR. |
| “We couldn’t load your data” | Retry, then refresh. |
| Someone on your team is suspended | They can only read Memos and Resources. Talk to HR; you cannot lift it. |
| You are on a PIP banner | You keep full access. Talk to your lead and HR. |
| AVA down | Use this guide, or email `hr@afrivate.org`. |
