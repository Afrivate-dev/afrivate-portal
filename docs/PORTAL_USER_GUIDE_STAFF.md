# AfriVate Team Space — Staff Portal Guide

**Product:** AfriVate Team Space  
**Live site:** [portal.afrivate.org](https://portal.afrivate.org)  
**Audience:** Team members (ordinary staff)  
**Last updated:** 14 August 2026  
**Document code:** AFRI-PUG-02  
**Branded PDF:** [docs/official/policies/Afrivate-Portal-User-Guide-Staff.pdf](official/policies/Afrivate-Portal-User-Guide-Staff.pdf)

This guide is **only for team members**. It covers what you can see and do. It does **not** cover Admin, recruitment, appraisals, or other People & Culture tools — you will not see those in the portal.

If you are a **team lead** or **assistant lead**, use [AFRI-PUG-03](PORTAL_USER_GUIDE_TEAM_LEAD.md) instead. People & Culture and Administrators use the full [AFRI-PUG-01](PORTAL_USER_GUIDE.md).

---

## 1. What this portal is for you

AfriVate Team Space is the official work website. You use it to:

- Keep a to-do list and mark work complete
- Request time off
- Send a weekly update
- Submit proof that you finished an Alison course
- Fill in surveys
- Keep your personal details up to date
- Read memos and policies
- Thank a colleague
- Ask AVA how to do something

**The golden rule:** If it needs a record (leave, weekly update, certificate, “I have read this policy”), do it in the Portal. Slack is for chat. WhatsApp is only for informal or emergency contact. A Slack or WhatsApp message is **not** an official leave request or weekly update.

| Tool | You use it for |
|------|----------------|
| **Portal** | Records: leave, tasks, weekly updates, learning proof, surveys, shout-outs, your details, reading memos and policies. |
| **Slack** | Day-to-day questions and coordination. Reply to official Slack messages within **four (4) hours** on official work days. |
| **Email (`hr@afrivate.org`)** | Formal messages from People & Culture. |
| **Alison** | Taking the assigned course. Proof of completion goes in the Portal. |
| **WhatsApp** | Informal or emergency only — never leave, policies, or appraisals. |
| **AVA** | “How do I…?” She can open the right page. She never submits anything for you. |

You will **not** see an **Admin** item in the menu. That is normal.

## 2. Getting in

**Website:** [portal.afrivate.org](https://portal.afrivate.org)

### Request access (first time)

1. On the login screen, click **Request access**.
2. Enter your name, work email, and a password (at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, and one number or symbol).
3. Choose your department and type your job title.
4. Submit. You wait until People & Culture approves you. Until then you may see a “waiting for approval” screen.

### Sign in

Use your email and password, or ask for a **magic link** (an email link that signs you in without typing the password that time).

### Forgot password / change email later

- **Forgot password** is on the login screen. Open the email, set a new password, sign in.
- After you are in: click your name (top-right) → **Account & security** to change email or password.
- **Sign out** is in the same menu.

## 3. Finding your way around

### On a computer — left menu

You will see: **Home · My work · Inbox · People · Search · Weekly update · Getting started · Notes · Memos · Resources · Calendar**.

You will **not** see **Admin**.

### On a phone

The bottom bar shows **Home**, **My work**, **Inbox**, **People**, and **More**. Tap **More** for the rest.

### The top bar (every page)

- Sun / moon — light or dark screen
- Search
- **Availability** — tell colleagues you are Available, Away, Busy, or Focusing
- Bell — Inbox (red dot = unread)
- Your photo — **My profile**, **Account & security**, **Sign out**

A banner may offer **Install AfriVate Portal** so it sits on your home screen like an app. You can ignore it.

The purple **AVA** button is bottom-right (on a phone, just above the bottom menu).

## 4. Home

This is the first page after you sign in. It greets you and shows what needs attention.

**You will see**

- Buttons: **New task**, **Request leave**, **Submit check-in**
- How many tasks are due today, unread memos, and events in the next 7 days
- Coloured banners if you have an open survey, a learning certificate due, or a policy you have not yet acknowledged
- If you joined in the last 30 days: a Getting started progress bar
- Today’s events and the latest memos

**What to do:** Start here each work day. Tap a banner to go do the thing. The banner goes away when you actually complete it.

You will **not** see a number for “leave waiting on you to approve.” That is for leads.

---

## 5. My work (tasks)

Your to-do list. You can create tasks, assign people, and mark work complete. The portal stores **who** completed a task and **when**.

**Views:** Board (Up next → In progress → Complete / Stuck), List (by due date), Week.

**How to add a task**

1. Click **New task**.
2. Give it a title. Add a description, people, due date, priority, category, hours, and blockers if you like.
3. Save.

**How to finish work**

Open the task. Move it **Up next → In progress → Complete**. If you are blocked, mark **Stuck** and write why. Marking **Complete** in the Portal is the official record — do not only say so on Slack.

---

## 6. Inbox

Not email. Alerts **for you**: a shout-out, a task assigned to you, a mention, your leave approved or declined, a survey reminder, a new memo, or your access being approved.

Tap an item to open the related page (it marks as read). Use **Mark all as read** to clear the red dot.

---

## 7. Search

Type a name or word. You can find people, memos, tasks you can see, notes you can see, documents you are allowed to open, events, your leave, shout-outs, surveys, learning, and your OKRs.

You will **not** see HR-only files, other people’s private leave (except what the directory shows as time off), recruitment, or employee files. That is by design.

---

## 8. Weekly update

Every week, tell the team:

1. What you **finished**
2. What is **next**
3. Anything **blocking** you
4. How many **hours** you worked

Choose who can see it: your **department**, or **everyone**.

Open **This week**, fill it in, submit. You can edit the same week later. **History** shows past weeks.

You will **not** see a **Team** tab. That is for leads. Your lead reads your update from their Team tab.

---

## 9. Getting started

Mainly for new joiners. Watch the welcome **videos** and tick the **checklist**. Home shows video progress for your first 30 days. Some checklist items tick themselves when you do the related Portal action (for example a weekly update).

You cannot add or remove videos. People & Culture manage those.

---

## 10. Notes

Shared pages for meeting notes and how-we-do-things.

Create a page, write in it, and choose who can see it: **Private**, **Workspace** (everyone), a **department** or **team**, or named people. Delete only when you are sure — it disappears for everyone who had access.

---

## 11. Memos (read)

Official company or team updates. Open **Memos**, read them, and mark as read. Home reminds you about unread ones.

You **cannot** publish, edit, or delete memos. Team leads and People & Culture do that. If you need something announced, ask your lead.

If your account is ever **suspended**, Memos is still readable (along with Resources).

---

## 12. Resources (policies and files)

Handbooks, policies, and templates you are allowed to see.

1. Browse or search.
2. Preview or download.
3. If a file says you must acknowledge it, click that you have read it. Home and People keep a yellow banner until you do.

You **cannot** upload or delete files. You will **not** see HR-only files, and you will **not** see files marked for management only (those are for leads).

If you are suspended, you can still read Resources.

---

## 13. Calendar (browse)

See team events. Click one for time, place, and who it is for. Events may be for everyone or for your department.

You **cannot** add or delete events. Team leads can.

---

## 14. People hub

Open **People**. Tabs: Overview · Time off · Shout-outs · Learning · Surveys · Growth · My info · Directory.

**Overview** is shortcuts plus the same reminder banners as Home, and recent HR digest memos. You will **not** see a team snapshot or a PIP & suspension list. Those are for leads.

---

## 15. Time off (request leave)

**This is the only official way to request leave.** WhatsApp, Slack, a phone call, or “I’m off tomorrow” is not an approved request (except a true emergency — still record it here as soon as you can).

| Type | Meaning | Typical allowance shown |
|------|---------|-------------------------|
| **Annual** | Planned holiday | 20 days |
| **Sick** | You are unwell | 10 days |
| **Emergency** | Sudden, unavoidable absence | 7 days |

Give at least **three (3) official work days’ notice**, except accepted emergencies. Hand over work and tell colleagues on Slack **before** you go.

**How to request**

1. People → **Time off** (or Home → Request leave, or AVA → Go to Time off).
2. Click **Request leave**.
3. Dates, type, reason. Attach a medical note or other file if needed (upload or Google Drive).
4. Submit. Watch **My requests**: pending → approved or declined.
5. You and your manager can comment on the request.

You will **not** see **Team requests** or a leave calendar of other people. Your lead sees those. Until the Portal says **approved**, you are not on approved leave.

---

## 16. Shout-outs

Thank a colleague in public.

1. Compose a shout-out, pick the person, write a short message, pick a tag, add a photo if you like.
2. Send. Others can like and comment.

You cannot manage the tag list. People & Culture do that.

---

## 17. Learning (Alison)

You take the course on **Alison**. You **prove you finished** it in the Portal.

1. People → **Learning**. Open the Alison link.
2. Finish the course. Save your certificate.
3. Back in the Portal: course name, completion date, upload the certificate (or pick from Google Drive).
4. Submit. Status is **pending** until HR reviews it. If they reject it, fix it and resubmit.

A Home banner stays until your proof is in. You cannot assign courses to other people.

---

## 18. Surveys

When HR opens a **Pulse**, **eNPS**, or **Onboarding** survey:

1. Open People → **Surveys** (or the Home banner).
2. Answer and submit. One response per person.

Your individual answers go to HR. Your lead may see **combined** team scores, not your personal answers. You cannot launch surveys.

---

## 19. Growth (your own)

People → **Growth**. This is about **you**, not your team.

| Tab | What you do |
|-----|-------------|
| **OKRs** | Write your quarterly aims and key results. Slide progress as you go. |
| **1:1s** | Meet your manager outside the portal. You will see whether they have marked this month’s 1:1 complete. You cannot mark it yourself. |
| **IDP** | Write your development plan and submit. If your manager leaves a note, you will see it. |
| **Feedback** | When HR opens a 360° cycle, complete the forms assigned to you (yourself, a peer, your manager, and so on). |
| **Milestones** | Tick your day 30 / 60 / 90 items when those conversations happen. |
| **Awards** | See quarterly award winners when they are announced. |
| **Speak up** | Raise a confidential workplace concern. HR receives it. Not for leave or IT niggles. |

You will **not** see other people’s OKRs, mark 1:1s for others, or review other people’s IDPs.

---

## 20. My info

Your personal record for HR. Keep it accurate.

**You can save:** preferred and legal name, personal email, phone, work location, country, date of birth, pronouns, LinkedIn, bio, skills, emergency contact, next-of-kin notes.

**You cannot change:** employment status, whether you are an employee / volunteer / contractor, probation dates, or payroll flags. People & Culture hold those.

Check the completeness percentage and click **Save my info**. If HR asked you to review your details, an amber banner stays until you save.

---

## 21. Directory

The staff list. Search, open a profile, copy an email. Edit **your own** card (photo, bio, phone, skills, LinkedIn). You cannot edit someone else’s profile.

Availability comes from the top-bar control (Available / Away / Busy / Focusing).

You will not see other people’s PIP or suspension badges. Leads and HR can.

---

## 22. AVA

Purple **AVA** button. She explains how to do things and can show **Go to…** (Time off, Learning, Weekly update).

She **cannot** submit leave, save a weekly update, approve anything, or see records you are not allowed to see. You always click the buttons yourself.

If AVA is down, use this guide or email `hr@afrivate.org`.

---

## 23. If you see a PIP or suspension banner

People & Culture may place you on a Performance Improvement Plan or a suspension. You will see a banner at the top.

- **On PIP:** You keep using the portal as usual. Talk to your lead and HR.
- **Suspended:** You can still sign in. You can read **Memos** and **Resources**, and use **Account & security**. Everything else is paused until HR lifts it. If you open another page, you are sent back to Memos.

You cannot set these statuses. Only People & Culture can.

## 25. A good weekly habit

1. Open **Home**. Clear banners (survey, learning, policy).
2. Request leave in the **Portal**, not WhatsApp or Slack.
3. Mark tasks **Complete** in My work.
4. Submit the **Weekly update** before the week closes.
5. Keep **My info** complete.
6. Use Slack for chat. Use the Portal when it must be on the record.
7. If you are lost, ask **AVA**, then do the click yourself.

---

## 26. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|--------------|------------|
| Waiting for approval | Wait, or email `hr@afrivate.org`. |
| Red banner: suspension | You can still read Memos and Resources. Contact HR if this looks wrong. |
| Amber banner: PIP | Keep using the portal. Talk to your lead and HR. |
| “We couldn’t load your data” | Click Retry, then refresh if needed. |
| Magic / reset link expired | Request a new link from the login screen. |
| AVA does not answer | Use this guide in Resources, or email HR. |

You cannot approve leave, publish memos, add calendar events, open Admin, or change anyone’s role. If you need those things done, ask your **lead** or **People & Culture**.
